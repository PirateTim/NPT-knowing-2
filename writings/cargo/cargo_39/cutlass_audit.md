## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim Deconstruction
1. **Causal Proposition 1 (The Asymmetry of Capability Destruction):**  
   *Claim:* Vendor marketing enables credentialed managers to terminate skilled workers in favor of non-functional AI; once the subsidized infrastructure collapses, the human talent cannot be recovered, leaving an unbridgeable capability void.  
   *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* Empirically falsifiable. This claim would be falsified if enterprise AI deployments reliably achieved end-to-end task execution without human remediation, or if laid-off workers could be frictionlessly recalled without loss of tacit, situational knowledge. In empirical reality, enterprise surveys consistently document 95% pilot failure rates alongside permanent institutional deskilling, fully supporting the proposition.
2. **Causal Proposition 2 (Macro-Economic Concentration & Bubble Insolvency):**  
   *Claim:* The modern tech market is held hostage by a narrow cartel of foundation model developers whose capital expenditure vastly outstrips commercial revenues, creating systemic macroeconomic fragility.  
   *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* Falsifiable across capital expenditure cycles. Falsified if hyperscalers demonstrate standalone net margin profitability from foundation model APIs independent of vendor cross-subsidies or balance-sheet venture injections.
3. **Causal Proposition 3 (Agnotological Contamination as Social Asbestos):**  
   *Claim:* The deployment of generative models is not a benign efficiency trial but the active injection of toxic, unverified synthetic waste into operational workflows ("asbestos in the walls"), creating an institutional cleanup liability that will span generations.  
   *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* Falsifiable by tracking error propagation and remediation costs within codebases, legal registries, and administrative archives. Supported by evidence of polluted databases and automated hallucinations that corrupt subsequent downstream training cycles and institutional records.
