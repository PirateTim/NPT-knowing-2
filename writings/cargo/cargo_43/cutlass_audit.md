## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Forensic Causal Claim Audit
1. **The Executive Predictive Claim:** *Generative AI is actively destroying entry-level positions and reshaping workforce composition due to superior machine capability.*
   - **Popperian Demarcation:** **Falsified.** The Yale Budget Lab established an empirical test: if generative AI were driving displacement, workers with high and middle exposure to AI tools would experience a measurable decline in their share of total employment across the 33-month window. The empirical data showed the workforce share across exposure tiers remained essentially flat. Furthermore, the rate of occupational change matches standard historical tech adoption baselines (1984 PCs, 1996 Web).
2. **The Macroeconomic Causal Claim:** *The collapse in tech job openings and hiring freezes correlates with monetary policy shifts (ZIRP cessation) rather than generative AI deployment.*
   - **Evidentiary Warrant:** **Robust.** The Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) data curve demonstrates that job opening contractions cleanly aligned with the Federal Reserve's benchmark rate hikes in early-to-mid 2022—months prior to the November 2022 release of ChatGPT.
3. **The Early-Career Deficit Claim:** *Scarcity of early-career positions predates generative AI and stems from credential inflation and structural demographic oversupply.*
   - **Evidentiary Warrant:** **Grounded.** The asset notes that non-AI structural factors account for historical divergences between degree holders and requisite labor roles, while a recent six-percentage-point divergence between cohorts remains attributable to overall labor softening rather than automated replacement.
