## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Audit of Causal Claims
1. **Causal Claim 1:** *Trade coverage treating Tilly Norwood as an actress gaslights audiences and normalizes the uncritical replacement of human labor.*
   - **Popperian Demarcation:** Falsifiable and empirically verified. By presenting automated rendering output through the editorial conventions of the celebrity profile (headshot, pull-quotes, personal pronouns, artistic intent), *Variety* systematically erodes the boundary between an authentic laboring artisan and an automated corporate asset.
2. **Causal Claim 2:** *The production and promotion of synthetic female actors is driven primarily by male studio executives seeking submissive female substitutes ("What do men want to see? An AI woman who will do whatever they want").*
   - **Popperian Demarcation:** Unfalsifiable psychologizing / inductive illusion. While the gender dynamics of synthetic digital avatars are sociologically relevant, reducing systemic capital reallocation, liability evasion, and labor replacement to individual psychological malice acts as an *ad hoc* immunizing stratagem. It obscures the structural corporate incentive to eliminate legal personhood and collective bargaining obligations from production balance sheets.

### Itemized Epistemic & Formal Fallacies Detected
- **Ontological Obfuscation via Anthropomorphism (CUTLASS-RULE-006):** Trade media and tech vendors conferring psychological interiority, emotional capacity, and personal agency upon a software pipeline ("feeling very real emotions right now") to disguise an automated marketing stunt as an autonomous human-equivalent actor.
- **The Guild Stenography Paradox (CUTLASS-RULE-026 / SHARED-HEURISTIC-014):** *Variety* treating corporate marketing copy as a legitimate interview subject. The journalist executes the guild standard of craft (procedural stenography: reporting what the entity "said") while completely evading material verification of what physical system generated the text.
- **Category Mistake (Rylean Fallacy):** Assigning the socio-legal category of "actress" and "interviewee" to an automated rendering script, conflating the representation of a human performance with the biological and legal human who performs.
- **Motive Projection / Genetic Fallacy (in Leishman's analysis):** Leishman projects speculative personal psychological motivations onto studio management ("rich men wanting submissive women") rather than forensically auditing the structural evasion of guild minimums, likeness licensing, and intellectual property theft.

## 3. Anti-Financial Reductionism & Sail Locker Verdict

### Deconstruction of Verification & Liability Evasion
A shallow economic reading concludes simply that Particle6 and Hollywood studios "don't want to pay human actors." However, the epistemic stakes extend far beyond immediate payroll savings:
- **Liability Severance:** Human actors possess civil rights, likeness protections, collective bargaining representation (SAG-AFTRA), and the legal capacity to withhold labor or sue for defamation and unsafe working conditions. A synthetic software avatar possesses zero legal standing; it functions as an automated liability shield between corporate producers and labor law.
- **Eradication of Provenance:** By conjuring an "actress" who has no childhood, no physical body, no training lineage, and no labor history, the studio breaks the provenance chain of performance. Performance is converted from a continuous, embodied craft into an opaque statistical pastiche aggregated from non-consensually scraped training datasets.
- **Media Complicity:** Trade journalism normalizes this provenance destruction by granting the software an editorial platform, ensuring that no human laboring agent need ever be acknowledged, credited, or compensated.
