## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim Deconstruction
1. **Causal Claim 1:** *Generative AI and technological substitution are the primary drivers eliminating graduate entry-level vacancies.*
   - **Popperian Demarcation Test:** **Falsified.** Chris Eldridge (CEO, Robert Walters) reveals the actual hiring mechanism: *"Companies just don't want to risk their capital right now. And therefore, they're just pausing the graduate intake. But that pause really has now lasted for two, two-and-a-half years... particularly when you look at the US market, what they're looking for when they say, is it new applicants to the market, the recent graduates, they don't mean someone that's coming out of university today. What they're really looking for is somebody with one or two years experience."* The structural refusal to train fresh labor predates the generative AI boom; firms are freezing hiring to protect short-term margins and exploiting AI hype as an ad hoc rationalization.
2. **Causal Claim 2:** *Junior professionals do not suffer cognitive offloading when using RAG tools if they dedicate adequate time to prompt engineering.*
   - **Popperian Demarcation Test:** **Unfalsifiable / Pseudo-Scientific.** The demonstration of the law firm's internal tool ("Percy") treats syntactic prompt refinement as an epistemological proxy for substantive legal analysis. Asking an LLM to generate interview questions and claiming *"prompting skills are improving along with your legal skills"* is an ungrounded category error that conflates prompt drafting with legal doctrine and evidentiary verification.
3. **Causal Claim 3:** *Because entry-level employees cannot be profitably billed out immediately, the logical economic equilibrium is for families to pay training fees to corporations or submit to 10-year indentured labor.*
   - **Popperian Demarcation Test:** **Inductive Illusion & Non Sequitur.** The claim assumes as an unalterable natural law that capital should bear zero pedagogical overhead for the reproduction of its own labor force. It treats a political choice to maximize quarterly margins as an inevitable historical return to feudal mechanics.

### Itemized Fallacy Scan
* **Narrative Misdirection (CUTLASS-RULE-002):** Reframing multi-decade institutional disinvestment in workplace training and structural corporate downsizing as an acute technological crisis caused by autonomous AI agents.
* **Technological Scapegoating for Systemic Failure (CUTLASS-RULE-008):** Acknowledging that the educational system and corporate pipelines are failing to produce critical thinkers, but attributing the resulting employment collapse to AI tools rather than institutional decay.
* **False Dilemma / Feudal Bifurcation:** Posing the future of work as a binary choice between total entry-level job destruction or the restoration of feudal indentured servitude and paid apprenticeships.
* **Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc:** Conflating a macroeconomic hiring freeze driven by cost-cutting and capital hoarding with the technological displacement of junior labor by AI.
* **Ontological Obfuscation & Verification Theater:** Asserting that lawyers "still read every line" while simultaneously firing the trainee cohort whose physical task was conducting that ground-level verification.
