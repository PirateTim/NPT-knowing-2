### Section 2: Popperian Logic Audit & Toulmin Causal Warrants

- **Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability**:
  The core causal claims of the asset are empirically **unfalsifiable** due to ad hoc immunizing stratagems. When confronted with the documented risk of cognitive offloading and junior deskilling, the law firm's leadership constructs a non-falsifiable operational defense: if the synthetic tool produces accurate work, the AI has successfully automated legal drudgery; if the tool produces hallucinated or superficial errors, the fault is shifted entirely to the human operator for failing to "take time to actively produce the prompt." By defining prompt composition as an unmeasurable proxy for legal rigor, the asset insulates the technological substitution from empirical refutation.

- **Primary Causal Claim**:
  Deploying Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) software ("Percy") to bypass primary document review eliminates useless legal "drudgery" without degrading legal competence, allowing trainees to jump straight to higher-value strategic analysis.

- **Data / Grounds**:
  Simmons & Simmons' deployment of "Percy" to extract complaint themes and auto-generate witness interview questions, coupled with a partner's self-attestation that they "still read all the documents" and that prompting skill directly scales legal skill.

- **Warrant**:
  Document review and factual cross-examination during discovery are purely administrative, mechanical tasks whose cognitive benefits plateau after the third or fourth repetition; therefore, eliminating tactile engagement with the evidentiary record does not compromise the formation of professional legal judgment.

- **Flawed Backing**:
  The unexamined assumption that legal judgment (knowing *how*) can be decoupled from tactile factual discovery (knowing *what*), treating document digestion as fungible data crunching rather than the epistemic crucible where tacit knowledge is formed.

- **Structural Rebuttal**:
  If legal expertise is primarily an emergent property of tactile friction with unstructured human reality (the contradictory evidentiary record in full), junior practitioners insulated by RAG summaries will suffer from deep cognitive offloading and synthetic context blindness, rendering them structurally incapable of detecting algorithmic misrepresentations or conducting rigorous independent verification.

### Section 3: Classical & Epistemic Fallacy Scan

1. **Technological Scapegoating for Systemic Failure (CUTLASS-RULE-008)**:
   The narrative frames the sudden collapse of entry-level hiring as an acute technological crisis driven by generative AI, deliberately obscuring that corporate employers have maintained a multi-year capital strike and systematically dismantled internal training infrastructure long before LLMs were deployed.
2. **Tacit Knowledge Erasure (Failure Mode #12 / Epistemic Taxonomy)**:
   The explicit assertion that reading through "reams and reams of documents" is mere mechanical drudgery that can be outsourced to a RAG pipeline without losing the intuition, lateral pattern matching, and tacit contextual judgment acquired only through unmediated documentary friction.
3. **Equivocation / Category Mistake (Prompt Engineering as Legal Mastery)**:
   The conflation of prompt craft ("refining what output you want") with substantive legal reasoning and empirical verification. The asset equates linguistic manipulation of a transformer model with forensic interrogation of material evidence.
4. **False Dilemma / Agnotological Normalization (The Indentured Servitude Float)**:
   Host Isabel Berwick posits a false binary: either corporations abandon graduate recruitment entirely, or society must accept the resurrection of medieval master-apprentice fees and 10-year indentured labor floats. This normalizes exploitative, regressive labor structures as unavoidable technological consequences.
5. **Appeal to Unverifiable Consensus (Bandwagon Fallacy)**:
   Recruiting CEOs assert without citation that "probably with AI, we can do 80 per cent of what junior lawyers do," converting vendor marketing projections and speculative executive desires into settled empirical fact.
