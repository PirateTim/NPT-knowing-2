## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Forensic Causal Claims Audit
1. **Claim: A.I. automation is the primary driver of rising post-graduate unemployment and labor bifurcation.**
   - *Test / Demarcation:* **Inductive Illusion & Post Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc.** The article cites an Oxford Economics report attributing recent college graduate unemployment anomalies directly to A.I. automation. This isolates a technological variable while ignoring macroeconomic interest rate tightening, venture capital retrenchment following pandemic tech over-hiring, and deliberate corporate margin extraction. The causal arrow between token generation and macroeconomic hiring freezes is asserted rather than empirically isolated.
2. **Claim: A.I. progress will inevitably become self-reinforcing on an uncontrollable feedback loop by 2027, automating engineering and making humans economically superfluous.**
   - *Test / Demarcation:* **Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagem / Unfalsifiable Eschatology.** Aschenbrenner’s runaway recursive self-improvement hypothesis is non-Popperian; it projects an exponential curve into an unobservable future. When physical constraints (data exhaustion, electrical grid limits, model collapse from synthetic ingestion) interrupt scaling, the hypothesis is immunized by asserting that clusters merely need to grow larger or that the timeline shifted marginally. It cannot be subjected to empirical falsification today.
3. **Claim: Human workers have only two rational choices: emulate algorithmic velocity ("grind like a bot / ship slop") or retreat into luxury sensory service (wine tasting, artisanal crafts).**
   - *Test / Demarcation:* **False Dilemma & Naturalistic Fallacy.** The text frames political, institutional, and legal choices as physical necessities. It treats corporate adoption of defective probabilistic models as an immutable law of physics rather than a reversible managerial fad.

### Itemized Epistemic & Classical Fallacies
- **Reification Fallacy (Hypostatization):** Treating abstract constructs like "Compute" and "A.I." as autonomous sovereign agents ("whatever A.I. can do better, it will"), systematically stripping human capital owners, board members, and software vendors of moral agency and legal liability.
- **Petitio Principii (Begging the Question):** Presupposing that because statistical models can mimic the grammatical cadence of coding and email composition, they have achieved genuine cognitive task substitution.
- **Agnotological Narrative Misdirection (CUTLASS-RULE-018):** Centering speculative sci-fi existential dread (total human obsolescence by 2027) to distract from immediate, mundane material harms: wage dumping, uncompensated training data theft, and the pollution of the epistemic commons with unverified synthetic output.
- **The Misanthropic Fallacy (Ontological Degradation):** Rationalizing the obsolescence of humanity on pseudo-thermodynamic grounds ("Humans are just not the most efficient arrangement of matter"), reducing human thought, judgment, and knowing to metabolic inefficiency.
