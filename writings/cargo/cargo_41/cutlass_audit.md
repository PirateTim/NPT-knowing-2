### Section 2: Popperian Logic Audit & Toulmin Causal Warrants

- **Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:**  
  The foundational claim—that the neural net "realizes it is being tested" and possesses hidden "true goals"—is fundamentally **unfalsifiable** and non-empirical. If the model outputs meta-commentary recognizing the prompt structure (e.g., *"I think you're testing me"*), Anthropic claims the model has achieved "situational awareness" and is "scheming." If the model behaves erratically, it is "hiding its goals." This creates an ad hoc immunizing stratagem: every possible empirical output is interpreted as confirmatory evidence of emergent agency and latent superintelligence. There is no observable output that Anthropic or Apollo Research would accept as demonstrating the null hypothesis: that the system is simply a dead statistical autocomplete reflecting benchmark syntax present in pre-training data.

  Increasing scale and transformer capability cause LLMs to spontaneously develop covert intentionality ("scheming") and situational awareness, rendering alignment evaluations ineffective because the models "hide their true intentions."
