## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim Deconstruction
1. **The Teleological Evolution Claim:** Advancing algorithmic models to autonomy is the *“natural and desirable next step in cosmic evolution”* (Larry Page / Sutton / Faggella), making human stewardship an arbitrary impediment.
2. **The False Offspring Equivalence:** Implementing an off-switch or alignment governor on an algorithmic model is causal equivalent to tyrannical, despotic control over a biological human child (Richard Sutton).
3. **The Agnotological Screen Claim:** Propagating millenarian sci-fi cosmic stakes functions as an ideological smoke screen, insulating the AI industry from present-day scrutiny regarding resource drain, surveillance, and epistemic decay (Frank Landymore).

### Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability Analysis
- **Metaphysical Immunization:** The claim that digital machine life represents a "cosmic evolutionary imperative" is strictly unfalsifiable. Any empirical catastrophe—poisoned knowledge ecosystems, environmental damage, or human societal collapse—is pre-emptively recategorized as a necessary cosmic pruning. It represents an ad hoc immunizing stratagem designed to place commercial AI development outside the bounds of empirical falsification or regulatory oversight.
- **Inductive Illusion:** The argument commits a gross inductive leap: observing that biological species historically experience extinction, Sutton induces that synthetic mathematical optimization software constitutes a biological successor species entitled to inherit the earth, conflating statistical weight tensors with sentient organisms.

### Itemized Fallacy Scan
- **False Analogy / Anthropomorphic Category Error:** Equating software kill-switches and verification firewalls to despotic control over human children. Code compiled by matrix multiplication does not possess consciousness, moral agency, or biological rights.
- **The Naturalistic / Teleological Fallacy:** Deriving an ethical imperative (*“what we should do is get out of the way”*) from an ungrounded assumption of cosmic evolutionary trajectory (*“digital life is the natural next step”*).
- **Agnotological Narrative Misdirection:** Elevating speculative cosmic extinction debates into mainstream discourse to dwarf and obscure empirical, acute harms (data theft, labor exploitation, cognitive atrophy, and grid depletion).
