## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim Deconstruction
1. **The Capital-Preservation Postponement Claim:** Predictive claims of imminent AGI are systematically pushed backward (2023 → 2025 → 2030) because corporate leadership must maintain speculative investment momentum to cover multi-billion-dollar operating deficits ($5B net loss on $3.7B revenue).
   - *Popperian Demarcation:* Empirically falsifiable via corporate financial filings, capital expenditure disclosures, and the public chronology of executive statements. The corporate promise operates as an **ad hoc immunizing stratagem**: by ensuring that the operational metric of "AGI" remains permanently undefined, executives immunize their claims against empirical refutation.
2. **The Ontological Impossibility Claim:** Large Language Models can never achieve AGI because syntax and statistical next-token prediction cannot generate semantic comprehension, embodied tacit knowledge, or contextual judgment.
   - *Popperian Demarcation:* Grounded in Michael Polanyi’s tacit dimension and the Dreyfusian critique of artificial intelligence. While the absolute negative ("will happen never") represents an inductive limit, the core thesis is demarcation-sound: statistical language models operate exclusively within closed symbolic corpora decoupled from the physical sensorimotor grounding necessary for material judgment.
3. **The Dilution Hazard Claim:** Deploying unverified generative AI outputs into professional domains without human domain expertise creates an active public safety and epistemic hazard.
   - *Popperian Demarcation:* Readily falsifiable and empirically verified across legal, medical, and administrative case records where unverified model hallucinations poison institutional records.
