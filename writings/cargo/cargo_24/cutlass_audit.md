## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claims & Demarcation
- **Causal Claim 1:** *Autonomous agents and AI tools cause digital burnout and productivity loss because companies deploy them with "sloppy execution" and a "lack of infrastructure," rather than because probabilistic token generators are inherently ungrounded.*  
  - *Popperian Audit:* **Non-Falsifiable Ad Hoc Immunization.** The article constructs a classic immunizing stratagem: the tools are inherently revolutionary, and any failure is tautologically attributed to "poor implementation," "rushing," or lack of "sandboxing." If an implementation succeeds, the AI is praised; if it produces chaos and burnout, management was simply "unprepared." This insulates probabilistic architectures from empirical disconfirmation.
- **Causal Claim 2:** *Worker exhaustion is caused by a missing "use case" and inadequate corporate guidelines.*  
  - *Popperian Audit:* **Inductive Illusion & Attribution Substitution.** Exhaustion is not caused by workers staring confusedly at an interface wondering what it is for; it is directly caused by the physical labor of reading, untangling, and correcting synthetic "workslop" that mimics authoritative prose while containing factual or functional nullities.

### Itemized Epistemic & Formal Fallacies
- **Reification Fallacy (Metaphoric Laundering):** Reifying systemic managerial failure and computational error into a financial instrument ("AI debt"). Calling corporate epistemic decay a "debt" falsely implies it is a quantifiable, manageable asset-liability calculation that can be settled financially, rather than an irreversible pollution of organizational context and trust.
- **Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagem ("No True Scotsman"):** Henry Ajder asserts that "CTOs or innovation officers, the good ones I've worked with... aren't sugar coating the disruption." This rhetorically divorces failure from the technology by asserting that competent leaders will magically navigate "bumps in the road," leaving systemic technical defects unexamined.
- **Shifting the Burden of Verification:** The operational reality documented by the text is that the producer of synthetic text/code incurs zero cost to generate unverified claims, while the downstream recipient bears the full cognitive and temporal penalty of auditing and debunking it.
- **Technocratic Solutionism (Paradigm-Constrained Remediation):** Enterprise management software vendors (Asana) diagnosing the pathology of software-driven overload in order to position their own workflow tracking software as the indispensable antidote.
