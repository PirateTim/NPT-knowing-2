## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim 1: Unprecedented AI data center electricity demand forces structural rate increases and grid instability on captive civilian populations.
- **Evidentiary Warrant:** The North American Electric Reliability Corporation (NERC) 2025 State of Reliability warns of "lower system stability" due to single-site interconnection requests jumping from 200 MW to 2–5 GW. A joint study by Carnegie Mellon University and North Carolina State University estimates nationwide residential electricity rate hikes averaging 8% through 2030, surging beyond 25% in data center hubs like Northern Virginia.
- **Popperian Demarcation:** **Empirically Falsifiable & Grounded.** The causal link is mechanistically bound to utility capital expenditure recovery laws: regulated utilities are legally obligated to supply requested industrial load ("obligation to serve") and pass the capital costs of new generation, transmission lines, and sub-stations directly to all rate-payers across the rate base.

### Causal Claim 2: Corporate ESG pledges ("Water Positive" and "Net-Zero Carbon") offset and mitigate the physical resource drain of hyperscale compute.
- **Evidentiary Warrant:** Corporate sustainability filings cited in the text contradict these marketing claims. Google’s 2025 Environmental Report reveals a 51% surge in greenhouse gas emissions from 2019 to 2024. Furthermore, data centers consume potable municipal water through evaporative cooling towers, permanently venting local aquifer reserves into the atmosphere while counting distant, out-of-basin wetland restoration or stormwater projects as compensatory "water positive" accounting credits.
- **Popperian Demarcation:** **Unfalsifiable / Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagem.** The corporate thesis relies on an agnotological shell game where localized, non-fungible physical water tables are treated as fungible financialized offsets. Evaporating water from a Pennsylvania or Iowa aquifer cannot be physically remediated by funding water conservation in another watershed.

### Causal Claim 3: Hyperscale AI data center campuses bring robust, transformational economic development to host municipalities.
- **Evidentiary Warrant:** Meta's 2,250-acre, $10 billion "Hyperion" project in rural Richland Parish, Louisiana, permanently converts farmland to generate an estimated 500 permanent operational positions—equivalent to the staff of a single modest community hospital—while receiving massive state sales tax exemptions that strip the municipal tax base.
- **Popperian Demarcation:** **Inductive Illusion & Misleading Equivocation.** Massive capital expenditure ($10B) is conflated with broad-based economic prosperity. Hyperscale facilities are highly automated, capital-intensive, unpeopled industrial footprints whose long-term maintenance costs and utility strains fall on local residents while the economic rents flow exclusively to Silicon Valley equity holders.

### Itemized Fallacy Scan
1. **Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagem (The Fungible Commons Fallacy):** Tech conglomerates insulate their resource extraction from public criticism by inventing corporate accounting constructs like "water positivity" and "clean energy matching," pretending that global financial balance sheets can restore physically depleted local aquifers.
2. **Equivocation on "Economic Growth":** State economic development agencies equivocate between temporary, transient construction labor (which is often imported non-local contract labor) and permanent community employment, masking that hyperscale server halls operate with minimal human staff once online.
3. **Institutional Rent-Seeking via Monopoly Mandate:** Tech companies exploit the statutory "obligation to serve" imposed on public utilities to bypass normal competitive market pricing, forcing captive residential consumers to underwrite the massive capital costs of tech-specific baseload expansion.
