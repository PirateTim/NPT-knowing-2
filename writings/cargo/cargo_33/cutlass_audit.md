## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Audit of Causal Claims
1. **Causal Claim: Algorithmic opacity in platform labor is a legal construct, not a technological inevitability.**
   - *Epistemic Audit:* The claim asserts that the inability to audit task assignment, pay calculation, and disciplinary firing thresholds is enforced through trade secrecy, NDAs, and corporate misclassification rather than underlying computational complexity.
   - *Popperian Demarcation:* **Falsifiable.** If platforms were subjected to discovery and their disciplinary/wage algorithms revealed to be simple linear regression models, deterministic threshold gates, or arbitrary heuristics rather than uninterpretable mathematical manifolds, the claim is empirically validated. The asset provides concrete evidence: firms claim proprietary secrecy over basic performance quotas.
2. **Causal Claim: Monopsony labor exploitation in the Global South is the material precondition for consumer AI safety and functionality in the Global North.**
   - *Epistemic Audit:* The platform economy links consumer harm and worker exploitation through a shared algorithmic machinery: user safety filters rely on the psychological degradation of underpaid moderators filtering graphic violence and hate speech for eight hours a day.
   - *Popperian Demarcation:* **Falsifiable.** Supported empirically by Equidem's fieldwork (*Scroll. Click. Suffer.*) documenting 113 data labelers across four nations whose outsourced labor directly trains and moderates models for OpenAI, Meta, and ByteDance.
3. **Causal Claim: State resistance to labor oversight (e.g., U.S. resistance at the ILO) maintains a deliberate regulatory void to preserve corporate monopsony power.**
   - *Epistemic Audit:* Institutional actors actively police regulatory boundaries to prevent algorithmic management from being recognized under labor standards.
   - *Popperian Demarcation:* **Falsifiable.** Verifiable through official diplomatic proceedings and treaty negotiations at the ILO.

### Fallacy Scan (Itemizing Target Systemic Fallacies)
- **Reification / The Pathetic Fallacy (Institutional Target):** The corporate and legal fiction that the "algorithm" is an autonomous agent or neutral market mechanism making decisions ("algorithmic wage adjustment"), which obscures the human executives and software designers who authored the governing rules.
- **Liability Severance / The Intermediary Shell Game:** The procedural defense that because work is mediated by code and executed through third-party subcontractors in Nairobi or Manila, the lead technology firm ceases to be an employer under labor law.
- *Evaluation of Bhattacharjee's Thesis:* Zero formal logical fallacies detected in the author's argument. The essay avoids technodeterministic panic and grounds its causal claims in verifiable legal mechanisms, labor contracts, and empirical NGO documentation.
