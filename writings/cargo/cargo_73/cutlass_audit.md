1. **Causal Claim 1:** Programmatic API distillation by Alibaba directly "cloned [Claude's] smartest capabilities" and "reasoning methods."
   - *Popperian Audit:* **Unfalsifiable / Speculative.** The asset offers no empirical technical benchmark comparing Alibaba’s internal models before and after the alleged 28.8 million queries.
