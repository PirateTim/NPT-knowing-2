## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Audit of Causal Claims & Demarcation
1. **Claim 1: The collapse of public trust and hardening of political polarization is driven by novel AI chatbots flooding the information ecosystem with unchecked content.**
   * *Popperian Audit:* **Non-falsifiable through post-hoc scapegoating (Technological Scapegoating for Systemic Failure / Paradigm-Constrained Remediation).** The collapse of institutional trust, partisan polarization, and the public's flight from mainstream sources long preceded generative AI (e.g., 2003 Iraq WMD access stenography, the 2008 financial crisis reporting, media consolidation, and institutional obfuscation). Attributing the crisis to LLMs immunizes the structural failures of legacy media and state institutions from empirical critique.
2. **Claim 2: An automated multi-agent AI Fact-Checker querying "trusted datasets" can restore shared truth and public trust as simply as a spell-checker.**
   * *Popperian Audit:* **Pseudoscience / Infinite Regress (Inductive Illusion).** What constitutes a "trusted dataset"? In an ecosystem where official organs and legacy broadcast networks routinely publish ungrounded claims and access-driven PR, querying an automated database of established consensus claims merely automates circular authority. If "Laura" verifies a claim against the very legacy outlets that manufactured the original ungrounded consensus, it introduces zero independent empirical falsification. Truth is not an orthographic typo; it cannot be resolved by an algorithmic dictionary lookup.
3. **Claim 3: Crises of trust in war zones and domestic democracies stem from a "lack of information," which can be solved by providing concise, authoritative factual summaries.**
   * *Popperian Audit:* **Defeated by empirical observation (Deficit Model Fallacy).** Institutional distrust does not stem from a quantitative deficit of facts, but from qualitative experiences of institutional failure, elite deception, and the systemic severance of provenance by credentialed authorities. Flooding the public with automated assertions from the same credentialed class exacerbates rather than resolves the epistemic crisis.

### Itemized Fallacies Detected
* **Paradigm-Constrained Remediation (CUTLASS-RULE-015):** The author documents a crisis that is the direct consequence of the systemic decay of his own professional class (journalism's access stenography, collapse of primary verification), yet frames the problem entirely as an external technological threat (AI) and proposes an automated technological fix operating wholly within the flawed, unexamined assumptions of his original guild.
* **Technological Scapegoating for Systemic Failure (CUTLASS-RULE-008):** Rebranding systemic institutional illegitimacy and media decay as an acute emergency created by synthetic LLM chatbots.
* **The Guild Stenography Paradox (CUTLASS-RULE-026 / SHARED-HEURISTIC-014):** Conflating the public myth of journalism (independent empirical verification of physical reality) with guild reality (access maintenance and procedural attribution of official power), evidenced by assembling network executives, military intelligence advisors, and PR figures to police "truth."
* **Category Mistake (Orthographic / Ontological Conflation):** Treating ontological truth-determination in complex political, military, and scientific disputes as mechanically equivalent to automated orthographic spell-checking.
* **Infinite Regress (Turtles All the Way Down):** Proposing an AI agent to verify AI-generated text by querying digitized text archives produced by the very journalistic guild that trained the hallucinating models in the first place.
