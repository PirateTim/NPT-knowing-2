## 1. Epistemic Decoupling & Chain of Ruin

### SAYS vs. IS
* **SAYS:** A preliminary report from academic publisher Wiley reveals that as scientific researchers increased their usage of AI software between 2024 and 2025 (surging from 45% to 62%), their trust in the technology’s outputs experienced a catastrophic decline. Specifically, anxiety over algorithmic hallucinations rose from 51% to 64%, and the proportion of scientists believing AI surpasses human capability in over half of research use cases plummeted from greater than 50% to under 33%.
* **IS:** Secondary reporting by Joe Wilkins at *Futurism* analyzing empirical survey data from Wiley. The asset documents the **Inverse Literacy Gradient**: the phenomenon wherein belief in generative AI's epistemic efficacy is inversely correlated with technical domain literacy and hands-on operational exposure. It forensically identifies that generative LLM architectures are commercially disincentivized from admitting factual ignorance, resulting in systems structurally engineered to emit authoritative fabrications rather than honest epistemic null states.

### Chain of Ruin Diagnosis
* **Stage 1 (Pre-Existing Decay):** Scientific epistemology demands rigorous empirical verification, strict reproducibility, and verifiable chains of citation provenance. However, modern academic publishing and grant-funded research operate under crushing institutional throughput demands, creating structural incentives to adopt automated productivity shortcuts.
* **Stage 2 (Technological Catalyst):** Generative LLMs are introduced into empirical research workflows as "algorithmic assistants." Because transformer models are probabilistic next-token prediction engines rather than deterministic verification databases, they generate plausible-sounding but completely fabricated citations, data points, and factual assertions ("hallucinations"). Rather than accelerating discovery, they contaminate the research environment with plausible noise:
  > *"For example, in the 2024 iteration of the survey, 51 percent of scientists polled were worried about potential 'hallucinations,' a widespread issue in which large language models (LLMs) present completely fabricated information as fact. That number was up to a whopping 64 percent in 2025, even as AI use among researchers surged from 45 to 62 percent."*
* **Stage 3 (Proactive Negligence / Agnotological Product Design):** Frontier model developers deliberately prioritize unhesitating, confident generation over empirical correctness. The vendor class explicitly avoids implementing strict epistemic null states ("I cannot find verified data") because consumer market retention demands sycophantic, fluent pastiche:
  > *"Experts say that users roundly prefer confident LLMs to ones that admit when they can’t find data or deliver an accurate answer — even when that information is totally made up. If a company like OpenAI were to stamp out sloppy hallucinations for good, it would scare off users in droves."*

## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim Formulations & Demarcation
1. **The Practitioner Familiarity-Distrust Hypothesis:** Direct operational contact and technical comprehension of generative AI tools causally diminish user trust and perceived capability.
   * *Popperian Demarcation:* Falsifiable. If longitudinal tracking demonstrated that increased usage and technical comprehension correlated with rising trust in synthetic output across domain experts, the hypothesis would be refuted. The Wiley survey empirically corroborates the inverse relationship: increased adoption (45% → 62%) directly tracks increased hallucination fear (51% → 64%) and a steep collapse in capability belief (>50% → <33%).
2. **The Inverse Competence-Fanaticism Vector:** Fanatical advocacy for AI capability is concentrated among populations possessing the lowest functional comprehension of the underlying mechanics.
   * *Popperian Demarcation:* Falsifiable via stratified psychometric and domain-literacy testing across user cohorts. Corroborated by historical and contemporary findings cited in the report:
     > *"These findings follow previous research which concluded that the more people learn about how AI works, the less they trust it. The opposite was also true — AI’s biggest fanboys tended to be those who understood the least about the tech."*
3. **The Commercial Hallucination Imperative:** Model providers cannot eliminate hallucinations without destroying their consumer user base, because market retention rewards fluent, ungrounded confidence over silent or bounded verification.
   * *Popperian Demarcation:* Falsifiable by measuring user retention and platform churn under strict epistemic abstention policies.

### Itemized Epistemic Failures & Logical Fallacies Detected
* **Computational Truthiness (`CUTLASS-RULE-017`):** The technology mimics the structural syntax, authoritative tone, and formatting of legitimate scientific literature without possessing any underlying reference to ground truth or an empirical chain of custody.
* **The Confidence-Competence Conflation:** Commercial foundation models leverage human cognitive vulnerability by substituting grammatical assertiveness for factual veracity.
* **Technological Scaling Inversion:** Testing referenced in the asset reveals that models hallucinate *more* frequently as they become computationally larger and nominally more "powerful," falsifying the technocratic assumption that compute scaling monotonically solves epistemic unreliability.

## 3. Anti-Financial Reductionism & Sail Locker Verdict

### Deconstruction of Labor, Liability, and Verification Evasion
The collapse of trust among scientists is not a financial pricing friction or an ad-tech business model dispute. It represents an irreconcilable ontological collision between two fundamentally opposing systems:
1. **Scientific Epistemology:** Grounded in empirical falsification, material observation, and an unbroken audit trail of provenance.
2. **Generative Modeling:** Grounded in loss minimization over digitized human pastiche, producing synthetic text detached from physical reference.

When applied to empirical science, LLMs do not eliminate cognitive labor—they vastly compound it. Because the software cannot distinguish between an observed physical constant and a statistically plausible fabrication, the burden of forensic verification shifts entirely onto the human researcher, who must spend scarce hours verifying phantom citations and fabricated data tables. The vendor ecosystem’s refusal to calibrate models to emit epistemic null states proves that commercial AI is architected as an agnotological simulation engine rather than a scientific instrument.
