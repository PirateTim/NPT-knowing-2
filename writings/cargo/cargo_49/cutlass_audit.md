## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim Deconstruction
1. **Causal Claim 1:** Generative AI's capacity to hallucinate citations and emit non-referential management jargon satisfies the operational criteria for leadership and partnership in elite corporate consulting firms.
   - *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* Satirical in form, but empirically grounded in the political economy of consulting. Falsifiable by auditing whether partner promotions and billable deliverables require empirical verification and factual accuracy, or whether advancement correlates with billing volume and procedural rhetoric. Historical audits (e.g., McKinsey's opioid modeling, Deloitte's automated reporting) consistently demonstrate that empirical veracity is secondary to client narrative confirmation and liability shielding.
2. **Causal Claim 2:** Corporate jargon ("leveraging holistic verticals for client-centric uplift") functions as an epistemic defense mechanism; it commands deference and prevents scrutiny precisely because its lack of semantic meaning renders it immune to empirical challenge.
   - *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* Highly falsifiable through linguistic and sociotechnical analysis. The text illustrates an ad hoc immunizing stratagem: an executive deliverable that "no one has fully read but everyone agreed 'sounded important'" is insulated from falsification because it asserts no testable empirical propositions.

### Classical & Epistemic Fallacy Itemization
- **Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagem:** The deployment of deliberate buzzword opacity ("leveraging holistic verticals for client-centric uplift") specifically structured to evade falsification, empirical measurement, or liability.
- **Category Mistake / Ontological Obfuscation:** The satirical personification of statistical next-token prediction algorithms as corporate actors possessing "strategic vision," "efficiency," and eligibility for legal partnership, mirroring the real-world tech industry's deliberate obfuscation of software as sentient agents.
- **Fallacy of Accent & Aesthetic Laundering:** Elevating syntactic polish over truth value—validating a report based on its "great use of the em dash" and typographic cadence while ignoring that its foundational citations are completely fabricated.

Generative AI is embraced not simply because it is cheap, but because it is an automated, non-human liability shield: it produces the procedural aesthetic of research (formal citations, academic names, complex terminology) without requiring any human being to commit their personal epistemic credibility to the underlying facts.
