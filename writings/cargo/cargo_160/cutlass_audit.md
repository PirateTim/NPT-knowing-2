## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Audit of Causal Claims
1. **Causal Claim A (The Provenance Fallacy):** *Licensing content to Perplexity ensures that users receive accurate, verified community information.*
   * *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* Falsified empirically. Large language models and answer engines do not transmit verified reporting intact; they parse, compress, re-weight, and probabilistically synthesize text snippets alongside secondary web noise. Once ingested into an LLM synthesis layer, verified local reporting is stripped of its bounded context, editorial corrections, and legal accountability. The citizen receives an algorithmic probabilistic guess attributed via footnote, not verified journalism.
2. **Causal Claim B (The Economic Trickle-Down Fallacy):** *Licensing agreements create new revenue that directly sustains newsrooms and frontline journalists.*
   * *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* Ad-hoc immunizing stratagem. The assertion that platform licensing revenue trickles down to sustain beat reporters contradicts two decades of digital platform history (from Google News to Facebook Instant Articles) and Gannett's own corporate behavior, where licensing revenues and cost savings are absorbed by corporate debt servicing, executive bonuses, and shareholder dividends while local newsrooms continue to suffer layoffs and consolidations.
3. **Causal Claim C (The Inversion of Preservation):** *Disintermediating local newspapers by feeding them into an AI answer engine constitutes "championing" local journalism.*
   * *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* Inductive illusion and Orwellian inversion. An institution cannot champion its labor by training its audience to bypass the institution entirely. By facilitating zero-click answers, Gannett accelerates the destruction of its own direct subscriber base and civic necessity.

### Itemized Detected Fallacies & Agnotological Failures
* **Orwellian Inversion (Equivocation):** Redefining the programmatic liquidation of a newsroom’s direct audience relationship as "championing local journalism."
* **Technocratic Capitulation / The Survival Fallacy:** Assuming that feeding proprietary ground truth into a commoditized computational blender preserves institutional relevance, when it actually commoditizes the reporter into an anonymous data labeler.
* **Agnotological Disintermediation (Epistemic Vandalism):** Active participation by media executives in severing the provenance trail. The reader no longer knows *who* verified the school board budget or *why* the local reporter spent three weeks in the courthouse; they only know that "Perplexity answered."
