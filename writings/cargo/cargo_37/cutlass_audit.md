## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim Deconstruction
1. **Claim 1: Hasty corporate AI adoption directly causes severe operational waste and unmanageable "AI debt."**  
   - *Epistemic Warrant:* Survey data cited from Asana (79% of global firms anticipating AI debt) and Corcoran’s direct observation of venture pitches.  
   - *Popperian Audit:* **Falsifiable.** The metric of "AI debt" (maintenance costs, unverified codebases, error correction overhead exceeding licensing savings) can be measured against traditional technical debt. Corcoran correctly identifies that adopting ungrounded statistical engines without verification pipelines generates compounding operational liability.
2. **Claim 2: Companies that do not embrace AI daily will inevitably be left behind (Mark Cuban).**  
   - *Epistemic Warrant:* Unsubstantiated billionaire assertion grounded entirely in tech-evangelist fatalism.  
   - *Popperian Audit:* **Non-Falsifiable Inductive Illusion.** This is an unfalsifiable teleological threat. It immunizes itself against current empirical failure by pushing the catastrophic consequence into an undefined future, ignoring empirical counter-evidence (e.g., NFIB finding 76% of Main Street firms thriving without it).
3. **Claim 3: Asking a generative chatbot for "competitive analysis" and "risk assessment" produces actionable business intelligence (Kelly Daniel, Lazarus AI).**  
   - *Epistemic Warrant:* Promoted via CNBC's "How To Use AI At Work" educational module.  
   - *Popperian Audit:* **Pseudoscience / Hallucinatory Substitution.** An LLM has no access to private, real-time market data or ground-truth balance sheets; prompting it for competitive analysis yields statistical pastiche and regurgitated web generalities, actively substituting genuine investigative intelligence with plausible computational noise.

### Itemized Fallacy Scan (Detected Only)
- **Argumentum ad Metum (Appeal to Fear / Coercive FOMO):** Cuban's assertion that non-adopters "risk being left behind" leverages existential panic rather than empirical productivity data to compel capital expenditure.
- **Temporal Advisory Pastiche (CUTLASS-RULE-022):** Corcoran’s core recommendation—do not invest in tools before having a defined strategy or understanding of ROI—is generic advice that has been repeated verbatim for 50 years (from mainframes to PCs, the internet, and ERP software), retrofitted with modern AI keywords.
- **Ontological Substitution / Computational Truthiness (CUTLASS-RULE-017):** Lazarus AI's prompt director advising founders to substitute rigorous human market research with ungrounded chatbot queries ("What's the competitive analysis here?"), laundering probabilistic text into strategic authority.
- **Reification of Negligence ("AI Debt"):** Framing the accumulation of broken, unverified AI code and erroneous data as an abstract, exogenous financial-like "debt" rather than the direct outcome of managerial dereliction of duty.
