## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Audit of Primary Causal Claims
1. **The Neoclassical Reallocation Dogma (Technological Compensation Claim):**
   - *Causal Assertion:* Technological advancement is the sole source of true economic growth; reducing labor inputs per unit of output automatically enriches society, driving aggregate demand upward and inevitably regenerating new, superior categories of human employment.
   - *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* **Non-falsifiable Inductive Illusion.** Stevenson invokes the mechanization of agriculture as an ironclad historical precedent, asserting that because farm laborers transitioned into manufacturing and services over two centuries, cognitive labor must follow an identical trajectory. When pressed to identify which new sectors will absorb cognitive labor when general-purpose models automate symbolic processing across *all* sectors simultaneously, Stevenson retreats into an unprovable metaphysical horizon: *"none of us can really forecast the future in quite the right way to say where will we find the bonds of reciprocity between humans."* A hypothesis that predicts labor regeneration without the ability to specify or test the conditions under which it could fail is non-falsifiable dogma.

2. **The Inevitable Cognitive Displacement Threshold (The 50% Unemployment Claim):**
   - *Causal Assertion:* Generative AI and robotics will inevitably exceed the capabilities of average workers, displacing at least 50% of the human workforce because human intelligence is no longer a unique comparative advantage.
   - *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* **Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagem.** Ford’s assertion that 50% of the workforce will be replaced relies on a moving temporal horizon. When reminded that his initial predictions of widespread automation were made 16 years prior without realizing this threshold, Ford immunizes the claim: *"Well, you know, that could be far in the future... I thought it might be 50 years before, you know, something like that happened."* The claim cannot be tested or falsified in the present; every failure of mass automation to manifest is neutralized by extending the predictive timeline into the indefinite future.

3. **The Data Dividend as Structural Compensation (Commodification of Epistemic Exhaust):**
   - *Causal Assertion:* Society can offset the loss of labor-based wages by establishing a "digital dividend" or "data tax" that pays citizens for the corporate ingestion of their behavioral and creative data.
   - *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* **Epistemic Category Error & Flawed Warrant.** The premise asserts that individual data exhaust has an intrinsic, separable market value that can fund human living standards. As Ford correctly notes in rebuttal, monetizing individual data generation creates perverse gaming incentives that destroy the epistemic fidelity of the data itself. Stevenson’s claim assumes that surveillance capitalism's extraction of unpriced public commons can be converted into a sustainable welfare state without destroying both the data market and human agency.
