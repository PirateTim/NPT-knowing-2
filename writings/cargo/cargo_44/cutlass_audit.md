## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Audit of Causal Claims & Epistemic Warrants
1. **The Opt-Out Safeguard Fallacy:**
   - *Claim:* Allowing users to input granular restrictions on their synthetic doubles provides meaningful protection against non-consensual deepfakes and misinformation.
   - *Popperian Audit:* Falsified empirically upon rollout. The article itself notes that users immediately bypassed basic watermarks and that LLM safety guards are routinely circumvented. Placing the burden on individuals to enumerate every possible scenario they *disapprove* of (e.g., mustard, politics, crime) is an impossible negative proof that immunizes the platform from systemic culpability.
2. **The Infinite Optimization Immunization ("Hillclimbing"):**
   - *Claim:* OpenAI's ongoing engineering ("hillclimb on making restrictions even more robust") causally moves the platform toward safety and stability.
   - *Popperian Audit:* Ad hoc immunizing stratagem. Framing persistent security breaches and epistemic vandalism as early data points on an infinite "hillclimbing" curve renders the platform's safety claims unfalsifiable; any failure is dismissed as an incomplete optimization step rather than an architectural design defect.

- **Trivialization / Absurd Diversion Fallacy:** Reducing the catastrophic systemic collapse of evidentiary video and biometric sovereignty to whimsical, frivolous lifestyle preferences (banning mustard, wearing a "#1 Ketchup Fan" ball cap).
