1. **Stage 1: Pre-Existing Decay (The Gutting of the Local Press & Metric Decoupling):** Long before generative scrapers were deployed, local news was systematically devalued, consolidated, and abandoned. 2. **Stage 2: Technological Catalyst (Automated Scrapers & Synthetic Truthiness):** The arrival of turnkey generative toolchains (WP Auto, Zimmwriter using Perplexity, Byword.ai, Gravity Write) serves as the industrial catalyst. ## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claims & Falsifiability Analysis
- **Causal Claim 1 (Domain Authority Hijacking):** Repurposing expired, historically trusted domains into automated AI scrapers causes immediate, algorithmic promotion in Google Discover and Google News, siphoning millions of organic views away from authentic primary human reporting.
  - *Popperian Demarcation:* **Empirically Falsifiable & Verified.** The claim is validated by traffic telemetry (WECB.fm drawing 4 million visitors in May after abandoning its Emerson College radio roots; Byword.ai documenting a 3.6-million-visit traffic diversion from a human competitor) and SEO tool feature sets explicitly marketing Perplexity-driven bulk generation (Zimmwriter).
- **Causal Claim 2 (Technological Scapegoating of Trust Collapse):** Generative AI is the primary cause of readers losing trust in the digital media landscape and high school educators returning to oral/paper exams.
  - *Popperian Demarcation:* **Inductive Illusion / Scapegoating.** While Hjálmtýsdóttir notes high school teachers are retreating to "pen and paper and oral exams," attributing this solely to novel LLM content misidentifies the root cause. The epistemic collapse was preceded by search platform monetization of ad-arbitrage content mills, the dismantling of regional news desks, and the programmatic destruction of origin verification. Generative AI merely automated the final liquidation.
- **Causal Claim 3 (Platform Oversight & Policy Efficacy):** Google’s spam policies against "expired domain abuse" actively detect and mitigate deceptive manipulation on Search and Discover.
  - *Popperian Demarcation:* **Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagem (Pseudoscience).** Google’s PR statement (*"While we don’t comment on actions taken against individual sites, when we identify violations of our policies, we take action"*) is structurally non-falsifiable and contradicted by reality. Discover repeatedly and algorithmically injected *Glass Almanac*, *African in Space*, and *Farmingdale Observer* into user feeds until external investigative reporting (*The Washington Examiner*, *Nieman Lab*) shamed the operators into pulling articles.
