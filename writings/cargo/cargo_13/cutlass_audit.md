## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim Deconstruction
* **Primary Causal Claim:** AI capability and economic competitiveness require unrestricted, un-minimized bulk data collection; therefore, enforcing data minimization necessarily halts AI technological progress.
  * **Evidentiary Warrant / Structural Logic:** Modus tollens weaponized as regulatory coercion: if data minimization laws remain in force, model training becomes impossible; because stopping training is framed as economically fatal, data minimization doctrines must be abandoned or diluted.
  * **Popperian Falsifiability & Demarcation:** The claim functions as an unfalsifiable technocratic dogma by equating *current brute-force, web-scraping transformer architectures* with the universal, immutable essence of computational intelligence. The claim is falsified empirically within the asset itself: architectural efficiency and curated datasets (exemplified by China’s DeepSeek and academic curation projects) demonstrate that high capability does not depend strictly on uncurated web-scale expropriation.
  * **Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagems:** Industry counsel constructs an immunizing distinction between "abstract privacy harms" (the non-consensual bulk harvesting of human thoughts at training time) and "specific commercial harms" (downstream discriminatory use in lending or hiring). By declaring upstream expropriation an "abstract" harm, industry actors attempt to immunize foundational data laundering from judicial or regulatory audit.
  * **Inductive Illusions:** The asset illustrates the inductive fallacy of the tech industry: assuming that because scraping the historical digital commons produced initial generative capabilities, scraping deeper into private, low-quality, ephemeral conversations will yield continued cognitive breakthroughs, rather than conversational rot and semantic collapse.

* **Naturalistic Fallacy (Is-Ought Fallacy):** Because generative AI currently ingests all reachable human data ("is"), the legal and ethical frameworks governing privacy must adapt and surrender ("ought" / "adjusting to the reality").
* **Technological Determinism / Inevitability Bias:** Framing LLM data consumption not as an architectural choice by specific corporations, but as an inexorable physical force to which human rights and laws must submit.
