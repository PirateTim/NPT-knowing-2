## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim 1: Decentralized Data Pods Overcome Platform Monopolies
- **Claim:** Deploying personal online data stores (Solid Pods) decouples user data from proprietary services, which will mechanically break the extractive power of platform monopolies and compel them to negotiate for data access.
- **Popperian Demarcation & Falsification:** **Falsified in Practice / Inductive Illusion.** The causal model presumes that platform power derives strictly from custodial possession of raw personal files, ignoring the systemic reality that platform dominance is driven by asymmetric capital, proprietary social graphs, network effects, and planetary-scale compute infrastructure. When subjected to empirical testing (the BBC pilot and the Flanders government initiative offering pods to nearly seven million citizens), the hypothesis collapsed: the BBC prototype never exited testing, and fewer than 1,000 citizens in Flanders actively use it. Platforms did not yield; users simply refused the unpaid administrative labor of managing individual data vaults.
- **Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagem:** When confronted with stalled enterprise and civic adoption, Inrupt pivoted from infrastructure to speculative AI consumer tooling ("Charlie"), asserting that *"it takes only one 'killer app' to vindicate a technology,"* thereby immunizing the failed protocol thesis from definitive empirical falsification.

### Causal Claim 2: Epistemic Collapse Is a "Design Issue" Solvable by Architecture
- **Claim:** The rise of algorithmic addiction, viral disinformation, and predatory extraction on the web is an engineering defect ("a design issue") in web protocols, which can be remedied by re-architecting data boundaries and implementing structured linked metadata.
- **Popperian Demarcation & Falsification:** **Pseudoscientific Category Error.** It conflates the political, economic, and institutional conditions of digital monopoly capitalism with mechanical protocol syntax. Re-architecting markup languages or data-storage envelopes does not disarm private corporate incentives to harvest attention or strip-mine copyright. Treating an institutional regime of agnotology as an engineering bug renders the premise non-falsifiable, as every societal failure can be excused as an incomplete implementation of the protocol.

### Causal Claim 3: Simulated Empathy Is Functionally Equivalent to Genuine Care
- **Claim:** If an automated computational system or language model can accurately simulate the behavioral markers of caring, it is operationally and philosophically indistinguishable from authentic human empathy.
- **Popperian Demarcation & Falsification:** **Unfalsifiable Anthropomorphic Axiom.** This assertion immunizes language models and platform operators from legal and epistemic liability. By equating statistical pattern reproduction with normative accountability, it removes empirical criteria for determining liability when systems inflict harm, defining epistemic and emotional simulation as self-justifying ground truth.

### Itemized Detected Fallacies
- **Managed Critique / Blunted Epistemic Fallacy (CUTLASS-RULE-001):** The author documents real systemic failures (Google Zero, copyright strip-mining, surveillance capitalism) but blunts their force by embedding them within a hagiographic, bucolic portrait of Berners-Lee's personal integrity, deflecting structural outrage into gentle narrative pathos.

### Deconstruction of Labor, Liability, and Verification Evasion

However, a rigorous forensic audit demonstrates that the core driver is not mere monetary accumulation, but the proactive evasion of verification and liability:
1. **Destruction of Audit Trails (DRM / EME):** When Berners-Lee approved DRM within W3C standards, the direct epistemic consequence was legally criminalizing independent security audits and reverse-engineering of browser code (under DMCA Section 1201), explicitly severing the public's ability to inspect and verify the execution of software on their own machines.
2. **Transfer of Administrative Burden to Exploited Users:** The Solid Protocol attempts to solve systemic data theft by demanding that individual citizens become unpaid system administrators of their own "Pods," masking the complete regulatory failure to hold extractive monopolies accountable.
3. **Epistemic Laundering via LLM Proxies:** Inrupt’s flagship AI product, "Charlie," does not build independent knowledge; it packages personal data and feeds it directly into proprietary commercial models (Anthropic's Claude). It acts as an automated liability shield—simulating personalized intimacy while locking the user into the exact predictive LLM ecosystem that is hollowing out the open web.
