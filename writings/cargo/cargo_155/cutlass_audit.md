## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claim Deconstruction
1. **Claim 1:** *"AI may cause the next global stock market crash – here’s how it could play out"*
   - **Purported Causal Mechanism:** The deployment, valuation, and capital absorption of artificial intelligence directly triggers a systemic macroeconomic liquidity collapse.
   - **Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:** Structurally unfalsifiable due to modal hedging (*"may cause,"* *"could play out"*). By framing the causal link as an open-ended future hypothetical, the assertion insulates itself against refutation; whether markets crash tomorrow, grind sideways, or inflate further, the modal statement remains immune to empirical falsification.
   - **Inductive Illusion:** Assumes that because market concentration around new technological infrastructure historically preceded equity corrections (the 1990s dotcom boom), current capital flows into AI compute must mechanically trigger an identical crash trajectory.
2. **Claim 2:** *"If top economists are to be believed, then a stock market bubble could be about to burst…"*
   - **Purported Causal Mechanism:** Consensus among macroeconomic authorities reliably indicates an imminent bubble burst.
   - **Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:** Lacks any operational demarcation criteria. The identity of the "top economists," their underlying macroeconomic models, and the boundary parameters for what constitutes a "burst" are omitted entirely.

### Itemized Fallacy Scan
- **Ontological Anthropomorphism / Reification Fallacy:** Treats "AI" (a suite of statistical matrix-multiplication algorithms) as an autonomous economic agent capable of causing a market collapse, obscuring the human fiduciary decisions and institutional balance-sheet mechanics that dictate capital allocation.
- **Technological Scapegoating / Fallacy of the Single Cause (*Cum Hoc Ergo Propter Hoc*):** Artificially isolates a single technological variable ("AI") and assigns it primary causal agency for multi-variable systemic market vulnerabilities (liquidity tightening, interest rate volatility, sovereign debt yields, regulatory shifts).
- **Appeal to Anonymous Authority (*Argumentum Ad Verecundiam*):** Validates the crisis narrative via unspecified *"top economists,"* substituting institutional prestige for verifiable empirical data or models.
- **Historical False Analogy:** Asserts an uncritical structural equivalence between the 1990s telecom/internet infrastructure buildout and present-day AI GPU cluster investments, ignoring fundamental divergences in balance sheet solvency, cash reserves, and corporate concentration.

## 3. Anti-Financial Reductionism & Sail Locker Verdict

### Deconstruction of Labor, Liability, and Verification Evasion

If the trillions poured into unverified enterprise AI fail to materialize as productivity, executives will claim they were victimized by technological volatility rather than held accountable for reckless negligence.
