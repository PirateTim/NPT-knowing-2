## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Causal Claims Audit
1. **The Inevitability Fallacy:** Tech companies are "steamrolling" Hollywood because "technology is moving faster than they can comprehend."
   *Forensic Demarcation:* Spurious causal attribution. The paralysis of studio executives is not cognitive deficit; it is calculated institutional opportunism. Generative AI offers studio leadership an asymmetric weapon against expensive creative labor guilds (writers, animators, VFX artists). Framing this as "moving faster than they can comprehend" launders proactive executive dereliction into passive technological victimhood.
2. **The Tool Equivalence Claim:** Generative synthetic video engines are merely "a new pencil to create with."
   *Forensic Demarcation:* Epistemically invalid and non-falsifiable. A pencil is a passive mechanical instrument requiring human motor execution and conscious conceptualization from a blank slate. Sora is an automated probabilistic inference engine trained on unconsented, pre-existing human cinematic artifacts that generates completed aesthetic scenes. Calling it a "pencil" immunizes the platform from liability and obliterates the distinction between tool and autonomous synthetic competitor.
3. **The Streaming Analogy Induction:** AI will follow the trajectory of YouTube, eventually resolving into a lucrative licensing platform for media companies.
   *Forensic Demarcation:* False historical induction. YouTube distributes and monetizes discrete, authored human video files with clear provenance and attribution. Generative models ingest and digest creative works into statistical weights, severing attribution and displacing the human creator entirely.

### Classical & Epistemic Fallacy Itemization
- **False Analogy / Category Error (The "Pencil" Fallacy):** David Ellison categorizes an autonomous generative diffusion model trained on millions of hours of copyrighted video as equivalent to a graphite stylus, erasing the massive data-theft infrastructure required for the model to function.
- **Performative Incantation (Equivocation):** Media executives repeating "we care about copyright like a prayer" while actively refusing to challenge the known unauthorized ingestion of their catalog by OpenAI DevDay presenters.
- **Techno-Determinist Fatalism:** The narrative frame that executives are helpless against an incoming "meteor," absolving corporate actors of their duty to enforce statutory property rights and labor agreements.
- **The Patronage Inversion:** Sam Altman framing the unauthorized scraping and commercial liquidation of human creative catalogs as a "gift to content creators" and "a new generation of fanfiction."
