## 2. Causal Claims & Popperian Demarcation Audit

### Audit of Causal Claims
1. **Claim: Enterprise AI ROI failure is caused by client implementation errors (expecting a "magic wand") rather than intrinsic technology limitations.**
   - *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* **Unfalsifiable (Ad Hoc Immunizing Stratagem).** If an enterprise deploys Scale AI's custom data pipelines and still incurs catastrophic hallucinations, legal liability, or negative ROI, the vendor's hypothesis is immunized by asserting that the client failed to incorporate "enough" expert feedback or misidentified the target problem. The claim defines success circularly: AI works when you "get it right," rendering failure proof of improper execution rather than model unsuitability.
2. **Claim: Incorporating senior domain experts into RLHF/fine-tuning loops reliably eliminates risk in high-stakes clinical and insurance contexts.**
   - *Popperian Demarcation & Falsifiability:* **Inductive Illusion & Non-Sequitur.** Micro-feedback provided by senior professionals on discrete sample outputs does not alter the underlying probabilistic, non-deterministic nature of transformer models. Treating piecemeal expert feedback as a guarantee of diagnostic safety commits an inductive fallacy, mistaking local alignment for universal epistemic validity.

- **Provenance Severance via Market Commodification (`CUTLASS-RULE-010` / Epistemic Taxonomy Mode 14):** Scale AI's core business model transforms qualitative human cognitive judgment into an anonymous, piece-work data commodity.
