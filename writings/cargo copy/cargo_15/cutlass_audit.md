## 3. Structural Logic Audit: Causal Claims & Evidentiary Warrants

### Claim 1: AI Overviews do not cannibalize publisher traffic or ad revenues because they expand the aggregate volume of search queries.
* **Stated Causal Mechanism:** Lowering the friction of asking questions induces users to search more frequently across more complex topics, which compensates for any individual reduction in outbound clicks.
* **Evidentiary Warrant Audit:** **FAILED (Non Sequitur & Conflation of Platform Metrics with Ecosystem Health).** Even if aggregate queries on Google increase, this metric measures Google's captive user attention, not outbound referral traffic to independent websites. An increase in internal zero-click queries provides zero economic or audience sustenance to the publishers whose content was ingested to generate the AI Overview.

### Claim 2: Independent publisher traffic collapse (e.g., 45% drops) is caused by organic consumer migration to short-form video, podcasts, and forums.
* **Stated Causal Mechanism:** Younger users inherently prefer TikTok, YouTube, Reddit, and podcasts over traditional written articles; Google is merely passively adapting its ranking algorithms to reflect these exogenous preferences.
* **Evidentiary Warrant Audit:** **FAILED (Causal Inversion & Victim Blaming).** Google unilaterally redesigned the SERP layout to push organic web links below the fold in favor of proprietary AI Overviews, Reddit partner modules, and YouTube carousels. Attributing the traffic collapse of text publishers to "user preference" ignores that Google actively re-engineered the discovery architecture to favor internal and licensed video/forum feeds.

### Claim 3: Replacing active research and web navigation with AI summaries is functionally equivalent to moving from library card catalogs to web search.
* **Stated Causal Mechanism:** Historically, card catalogs gave way to search engines without destroying literacy; therefore, search engines giving way to probabilistic AI synthesizers will simply free up human cognitive energy for deeper passions.
* **Evidentiary Warrant Audit:** **FAILED (False Analogy & Epistemic Erasure).** A card catalog or web search engine points the user directly to a primary text written by an identifiable human author, requiring the researcher to read, evaluate provenance, and cross-reference. An AI Overview replaces the primary source with an ungrounded, probabilistic derivative that conceals the lineage of the assertion, destroying the research process itself.
