### 1. Explicit Core Assertions

1. **Classroom Physical Device Prohibition:** The University of Chicago Law School has instituted a mandatory prohibition on digital devices (laptops, tablets, mobile phones) for First-Year (1L) core courses during the 2026–2027 academic year.
2. **Closed-Network Assessment Mandate:** Examinations for 1L core courses are restricted to in-person, closed-network evaluation environments.
3. **Oral Defense Requirement for Research Papers:** Upper-level Senior Research Papers (SRPs) require an in-person oral defense/discussion before faculty evaluators as a condition of completion.
4. **Sequenced Legal Research & Writing (LRW) Curriculum:** The LRW curriculum mandates a strict two-stage sequence: unassisted manual legal drafting precedes exposure to and training with generative AI output auditing tools.
5. **Upper-Level AI Auditing Integration:** Coursework in the 2L and 3L years, alongside a newly designated Law School AI Lab, focuses on training students to evaluate, audit, and verify AI-generated legal text and analytical outputs.
6. **Press Coverage Scope:** Trade journal coverage (Law.com) documents institutional policy parameters and stated administrative rationales without presenting empirical long-term performance metrics or independent post-graduation employment tracking data.

### 2. Evidentiary Base & Operational Parameters

* **Scope & Timing:** Effective for the 2026–2027 academic year across the University of Chicago Law School curriculum.
* **1L Core Controls:** Complete physical hardware removal (laptops, tablets, smartphones) during instructional hours; mandatory in-person closed-network examination settings.
* **Upper-Level Controls:** Mandatory live oral examination of research thesis, methodology, and doctrinal findings for Senior Research Papers (SRPs).
* **LRW Sequence:** Stage 1 unassisted manual drafting -> Stage 2 AI auditing and legal technology evaluation tools.

### 3. Toulmin Structural Mechanics

#### Structure 1: First-Year Instructional Isolation & Assessment
* **Claim:** Personal digital devices must be excluded from 1L classrooms and core examinations to ensure baseline cognitive skill acquisition.
* **Grounds:** LLMs instantaneously generate coherent legal analysis on connected personal hardware.
* **Warrant:** Unconstrained device access allows students to substitute automated text generation for personal analytical reasoning.
* **Backing:** Developing legal reasoning requires unassisted cognitive repetition in case synthesis and statutory interpretation.
* **Qualifiers:** Restricted to core 1L instruction and testing; does not apply to upper-level electives.
* **Rebuttal / Counter-Condition:** If digital network monitoring cannot guarantee hardware containment, physical device prohibition is required.

#### Structure 2: Senior Research Paper Oral Defense
* **Claim:** Written text submissions alone no longer serve as valid proof of authentic human authorship or comprehension.
* **Grounds:** Enterprise LLMs generate fluent long-form academic legal text and statutory citations without human cognitive synthesis.
* **Warrant:** Live oral cross-examination forces real-time verbal demonstration of subject-matter command.
* **Backing:** Automated text tools cannot perform live physical oral defense in response to faculty cross-examination.
* **Qualifiers:** Mandated for graduation-level Senior Research Papers (SRPs).
* **Rebuttal / Counter-Condition:** Inability to verbally defend written claims invalidates the evidentiary authority of the written paper.

#### Structure 3: Sequenced Curriculum Hierarchy
* **Claim:** Effective auditing of AI legal outputs requires pre-existing manual drafting mastery.
* **Grounds:** AI outputs contain plausible syntax but may embed hallucinated citations or flawed legal reasoning.
* **Warrant:** Detecting subtle errors in automated legal text requires domain knowledge built through manual research and drafting.
* **Backing:** Evaluators lacking manual drafting proficiency cannot distinguish correct legal doctrine from plausible machine error.
* **Qualifiers:** Governs LRW progression and AI Lab curriculum design.
* **Rebuttal / Counter-Condition:** Bypassing manual drafting instruction degrades the student's operational auditing capacity.
