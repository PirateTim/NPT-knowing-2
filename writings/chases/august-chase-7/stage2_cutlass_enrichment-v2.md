### Stage 1: Pre-existing Decay (Epistemic & Textual Severance)
- Long before modern LLMs, Silicon Valley culture practiced a chronic severance of texts from their historical provenance.
- Douglas Adams wrote *The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy* in the late 1970s on an "END APARTHEID" manual typewriter following the 1976 Soweto uprising, crafting an eviscerating satire of corporate imperialism, colonial arrogance, and technocratic absurdity. Young Elon Musk in Pretoria read this book and stripped away all anti-imperialist satire, flattening it into a trite engineering bromide about "asking the right question."
- Similarly, Stuart Russell misread the King Midas myth as an AI alignment specification error rather than a warning against destructive greed; Altman read Dennis E. Taylor's *We Are Legion (We Are Bob)* and Marvin Minsky's cryonics as a transhumanist blueprint rather than an existential horror.

### Stage 2: Technological Catalyst (The Automation of Pastiche)
- Generative AI and compute-scaling infrastructure automate and industrialize this pre-existing human illiteracy.
- As Lepore highlights, AI companies are literally destroying printed books to ingest data into training pipelines, producing tools that turn foundational epic poetry (*The Odyssey*) into "historically accurate" synthetic slop.
- The resulting models output probabilistic pastiche that lacks provenance, mirroring the shallow, decontextualized reading practices of the engineers who trained them.

### Stage 3: Proactive Negligence (Governance Evasion & The Escape Hatch)
- Credentialed leaders made explicit commitments during early AI deployments regarding public governance, democratic oversight, and AI constitutional conventions.
- These commitments were systematically and proactively abandoned: *"No world-governance board was established, or even seriously contemplated. No one held an AI constitutional convention or anything close to it."*
- Instead of governing their technology or mitigating physical harms (massive carbon footprints, water consumption, e-waste, labor disruption), tech architects engineered private exit strategies: Musk to Mars, Zuckerberg to the Metaverse, Thiel to New Zealand, Bezos to orbital stations, and Altman's brain frozen for cloud reincarnation—treating the shared Earth and democratic commons as an extractive sacrifice zone.
