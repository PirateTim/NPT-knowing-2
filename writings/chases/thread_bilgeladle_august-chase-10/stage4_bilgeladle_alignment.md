- **Mechanism:** Decades before large language models achieved scale, the tech-oligarchic class suffered an epistemic atrophy rooted in *The California Ideology* (Barbrook & Cameron, 1995) and the misreading of Golden Age science fiction. As Lepore establishes, figures like Elon Musk, Peter Thiel, and Jeff Bezos read Isaac Asimov’s *Foundation* not as a tragic critique of deterministic historiography (Hari Seldon’s psychohistory), but as a megalomaniacal entitlement to steer human civilization via mathematical calculation.
- **Epistemic Substrate:** The desire to bypass human agency, democratic friction, and rigorous scientific verification was already fully formed in Cold War cybernetics and neoliberal corporate management. The human subject was degraded into an interchangeable component (*Universal Tool* / *Human API*).

### Stage 2: Technological Catalyst (The Statistical Turn & Ingestion Flip)

The entire internet was compressed into a lossy, high-dimensional probability distribution (*Blurry JPEG of the Web*).

### Stage 3: Proactive Negligence (The Executive Manifestos as Regulatory Smoke)

### Detailed Rhetorical Analysis:
1. **Marc Andreessen (*The Techno-Optimist Manifesto*):**  
   Andreessen represents the purest distillation of *The California Ideology* fused with Marinetti-style Futurism. His text constructs a Manichean world where any friction, verification duty, or democratic accountability is branded as "stagnation" or "murder." He substitutes religious invocation (listing "Patron Saints" from Nietzsche to Moore) for any technical accounting of compute limits or physical hallucination rates.
2. **Sam Altman (*The Intelligence Age*):**  
   Altman adopts the cadence of a corporate messiah delivering non-denominational platitudes. His prose operates via *Texture Hacking*—presenting sweeping historical claims with zero evidentiary friction. He asserts that AGI will grant humanity "shared prosperity to a degree that is unimaginably great," completely decoupled from the material realities of data wall exhaustion (*Malthusian Scarcity Limit*) and the industrial consumption of non-renewable water and power.
3. **Dario Amodei (*Machines of Loving Grace*):**  
   Amodei borrows his title from Richard Brautigan’s 1967 countercultural poem without acknowledging its biting pastoral irony. His posture is that of the sober, responsible technocrat promising a "compressed 21st century" in biological research. However, this safetyist veneer hides a refusal to confront the core architectural limit: autoregressive LLMs lack physical world models, and predicting medical literature tokens is not equivalent to prospective biological ground truth.
4. **Mark Zuckerberg (*Open Source AI Is the Path Forward*):**  
   Zuckerberg wraps Meta’s commercial strategy in the rhetoric of populist liberation. By releasing model weights into the wild, Meta seeks to commoditize compute infrastructure while shifting the catastrophic verification and moderation burdens onto civil society. It is the operationalization of *Amnesty by Scale*.
