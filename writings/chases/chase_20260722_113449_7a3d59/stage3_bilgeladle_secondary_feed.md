### 1. Narrative Utility & Manuscript Alignment

This artifact provides the foundational epistemological framework for the transition from **empirical verification** to **probabilistic prediction**—a shift we term **The Statistical Turn**. In the narrative arc of *The End of Knowing*, this represents the intellectual surrender of the computer science and information management fields. 

Instead of building systems that map language and data to physical-world referents (semantic modeling, strict schemas, and relational integrity), the industry opted to project human language into high-dimensional vector spaces. This transition replaced the pursuit of *truth* with the optimization of *plausibility*.

#### Fleet Glossary Mapping:
*   **The Statistical Turn**: The philosophical and technical surrender of empirical verification in favor of probabilistic prediction, where "what sounds true" mathematically replaces "what is true" empirically.
*   **Computational Truthiness**: The illusion of authoritative knowledge generated by high-dimensional statistical models (LLMs) that produce grammatically flawless, highly persuasive text entirely divorced from ground-truth provenance.
*   **Epistemic Backstop**: The structural mechanisms—such as manual data curation, strict schema validation, and human-verified provenance—that prevent statistical drift from eroding the integrity of a knowledge system.
*   **Proactive Negligence**: The deliberate, cost-driven decision by credentialed actors to dismantle these Epistemic Backstops and offload cognitive labor to probabilistic engines.

### 3. Anchor Quote & Intellectual Lineage

To ground this transition in verifiable human intellect rather than algorithmic consensus, we anchor the critique of the Statistical Turn to a primary authority on causal reasoning:

> "Data is profoundly dumb. It can tell you that people who take aspirin have fewer heart attacks, but it cannot tell you *why*. It cannot answer a counterfactual question: 'What if we had not given them aspirin?' For that, you need a causal model, not just statistical associations. Connectionists [deep learning advocates] want to bypass the model-building phase and go straight from data to action. This is a mistake... You cannot understand reality without a model of reality."
> — **Judea Pearl**, *The Book of Why: The New Science of Cause and Effect* (2018)

#### Structural Importance:
Pearl’s critique serves as our **Epistemic Backstop**. It proves that the "Statistical Turn" is fundamentally blind to causality. By relying purely on high-dimensional correlations (vector proximity), generative AI models are mathematically incapable of distinguishing between a statistical pattern in their training corpus and a physical law of the universe.

### 4. Analogical Anchoring: The Vignette

To prevent this argument from drifting into abstract technological theory, we anchor it to a documented, physical failure of the Statistical Turn:

#### The IBM Watson Oncology Collapse (2013–2018)
In 2013, IBM partnered with the MD Anderson Cancer Center to revolutionize cancer treatment using "Watson for Oncology." Instead of training Watson on real-world clinical trial data and empirical patient outcomes, IBM’s engineers trained the statistical matching engine on synthetic, hypothetical patient cases created by a small group of doctors. 

Because the system lacked a causal model of human physiology and was entirely dependent on statistical pattern-matching within an unverified, artificial corpus, it began recommending unsafe and erroneous cancer treatments. In one documented case, Watson recommended prescribing a drug that causes severe bleeding to a patient who was already suffering from active hemorrhaging. 

The project collapsed after MD Anderson spent over $62 million, serving as a stark historical warning: **when you substitute probabilistic matching ("Computational Truthiness") for empirical clinical verification, the result is systemic failure and physical harm.**
