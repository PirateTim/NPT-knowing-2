* **Vector 1.2: The Shift from Strict Liability to Compliance Checklists:** Statutory frameworks were incrementally replaced by notice-and-comment administrative rituals and voluntary corporate social responsibility (CSR) guidelines. This pre-formatted regulatory bodies to treat "governance" as an exercise in box-checking rather than forensic verification.
* **Vector 1.3: Managerialization of Truth:** Academic institutions, particularly elite business schools, replaced rigorous empirical research with managerial frameworks and qualitative taxonomies, decoupling policy discourse from physical, legal, and biological ground truth.

### Stage 2: Technological Catalyst (Unanchored Statistical Scale & Epistemic Counterfeiting)
* **Vector 2.1: The Velocity & Opacity Gap:** The sudden commercial deployment of probabilistic large language models and predictive algorithms operating at global scale acts as an accelerant. The speed, opacity, and scale of automated decision-making engines completely overwhelm static statutory review mechanisms and administrative rulemaking cycles.
* **Vector 2.2: Epistemic Counterfeiting at Scale:** Probabilistic models generate fluent, authoritative-sounding text ("computational truthiness") without semantic understanding or data provenance. This floods regulatory and public channels with synthetic content, rendering traditional manual auditing methods obsolete.
* **Vector 2.3: Regulatory Friction Collapse:** Confronted with an overwhelming volume of automated outputs, oversight bodies experience cognitive paralysis. The sudden collapse of regulatory friction creates panic among C-suite executives and policymakers, opening a vacuum for turnkey "governance" solutions.

### Stage 3: Proactive Negligence (Substitution of Soft Self-Audit Rubrics for Verifiable Ground Truth)
* **Vector 3.1: Elite Academic Laundering ("The Prosocial Shield"):** Rather than demanding strict statutory prohibitions, data provenance disclosure, or empirical verification, credentialed policy elites (e.g., Knowledge@Wharton) actively construct voluntary self-assessment tools. The "Prosocial AI Index" serves as a performative shield, granting corporations administrative immunity while allowing ungrounded software deployment to continue unhindered.
* **Vector 3.2: Deliberate Evading of Malthusian Limits:** Corporate executives and academic advisors proactively ignore the physical realities of AI—Malthusian fuel exhaustion (the depletion of clean human ground-truth archives) and Data Decay Recursion (post-2023 synthetic data pollution).
