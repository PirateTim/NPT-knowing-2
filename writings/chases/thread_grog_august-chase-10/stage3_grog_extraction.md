### 3. Summary
Jill Lepore examines how prominent technology leaders—specifically Elon Musk, Sam Altman, Jeff Bezos, and Peter Thiel—invoke classic science fiction literature to conceptualize and justify their corporate objectives, including artificial general intelligence, space colonization, and cryonics. 

Lepore argues that these figures selectively adopt speculative concepts while disregarding the underlying satirical, anti-imperialist, or cautionary themes intended by authors such as Isaac Asimov, Douglas Adams, E. M. Forster, and Dennis E. Taylor. The essay details how narratives of planetary escape and machine labor are deployed alongside expanding physical datacenter and robotic infrastructure, while democratic governance structures for emerging technologies remain unbuilt. Lepore contrasts executive promises of technological transcendence against contemporary ecological loss, industrial automation metrics, and enterprise survey data on AI productivity.
