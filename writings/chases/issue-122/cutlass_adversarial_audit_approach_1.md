## 1. Executive Summary & Epistemic Verdict

2. **Vulgar Juridical/Economic Slippage in `Corporate Impunity`:** Lens 06 currently reads like an FTC white paper, copyright litigation brief, or antitrust complaint rather than a forensic diagnosis of verification abandonment and the destruction of information provenance.
3. **Popperian Demarcation Deficits & Boundary Leakage:** Severe boundary bleeding exists between `Institutional Stenography` (Lens 03), `Corporate Impunity` (Lens 06), and `Synthetic Warfare` (Lens 07), specifically around state vs. private information scrubbing and platform verification stripping.

## 3. Direct Adversarial Challenges to BILGELADLE

Bilgeladle must directly answer and remediate the following five challenges:

### Challenge 1: Purify the Compound Axis in Lens 01
You have mashed two unrelated dimensions into `Physical Depletion`: the physical thermodynamics of data center compute (energy, water, chips) and the informational limits of human text (Malthusian data exhaustion). 
* **Required Revision:** Re-anchor `Physical Depletion` strictly around **Thermodynamic & Material Constraints**. Move "Malthusian Data Limits" and the exhaustion of human text pools entirely to `Epistemic Counterfeiting` (Lens 02), where it directly feeds the recursive model collapse of the Caligula Loop.

### Challenge 2: De-Bourgeoisify and Re-Anchor Lens 06 (`Corporate Impunity`)
Lens 06 currently reads like a tech-policy blog obsessed with copyright lawsuits, licensing fees, and corporate profits. The Author's heuristic (`SHARED-HEURISTIC-002`) forbids defaulting to "they did it for money/clicks."
* **Required Revision:** Rename the focus of the lens definition from "theft and statutory damages" to **The Dissolution of Provenance & Legalized Immunity**. Enforce that copyright litigation is audited *only* as a symptom of the systematic destruction of human attribution trails and the institutional refusal to hold automated systems accountable to physical reality (`CUTLASS-RULE-010`, `CUTLASS-RULE-011`).

### Challenge 3: Resolve the Administrative Purge Conflict (Lens 04 vs. Lens 07)
You have created an irreconcilable ambiguity between the administrative deletion of datasets in Lens 04 and federal open data scrubbing in Lens 07.
* **Required Revision:** Codify an explicit **Boundary Demarcation Precedence Rule**:
  - If the action is an institutional/commercial removal, paywalling, or technological decay of historical records and cultural works $\implies$ **`lens_04: Archival Liquidations`**.
  - If the action is an active state-sponsored political purge, surveillance deployment, or ideological liquidation of human scientific witness capacity $\implies$ **`lens_07: Synthetic Warfare`**.

### Challenge 4: Establish the Anchor for Positive Epistemological Foundations
Where do assets discussing the historical baseline of high-signal knowledge systems (e.g., Chapter 5.3 on RSS, Wikis, Elasticity, Expert Amateurs, and Karl Popper) live?
* **Required Revision:** Explicitly specify how historical baselines and theoretical epistemology are handled. They must not be quarantined as Doldrums anomalies. Clarify that assets analyzing historical verification baselines anchor to the lens whose modern degradation they expose (e.g., Section 5.3 anchors to Lens 05 or Lens 03 as baseline contrasts), or provide an explicit Mainsail Theoretical Grounding exemption.

### Challenge 5: Calibrate the Doldrums Threshold ($\theta = 0.28$) Against False Rejection
Setting an arbitrary global cutoff of $0.28$ across high-dimensional space without empirical validation risks turning the Doldrums locker into an unmanageable swamp of hundreds of valid empirical specimens.
* **Required Revision:** Mandate that the Novelty Routing threshold $\theta$ be empirically calibrated against a 200-document stratified test set, and require that any document routed to `#unclassified_novelty` be scored with an automated feature breakdown showing its nearest two lens centroids and top contributing terms, preventing silent epistemic burial.
