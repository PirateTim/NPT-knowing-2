### B. Access Journalism & Roadmap Reporting Substitution (`CUTLASS-RULE-005`)

Speculative corporate roadmaps are presented as guaranteed historical inevitabilities.

### C. Regulatory Capture via Performative Philosophy (`CUTLASS-RULE-012`)
- **Preemptive Framing**: Manifestos are timed strategically to enter policy conversations before legislative bodies can codify mandatory accountability standards. By framing "openness" or "benevolent safety" on their own terms, companies dictate the parameters of future regulation.
