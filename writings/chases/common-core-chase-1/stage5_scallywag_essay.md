In 2008, state education budgets across the American republic faced catastrophic, double-digit revenue deficits. The RTTT rubric was weaponized with a non-negotiable prerequisite: states desperate for emergency federal stabilization funds were forced to adopt a single set of "college- and career-ready standards" sight-unseen—months before the CCSS text was even finalized or subjected to peer review. ### 1. The De-Centering of Human Interiority & The Misanthropy of David Coleman

Worse, Coleman instituted a bastardized version of literary New Criticism termed **"Close Reading within the Four Corners of the Text."** Students were forbidden from bringing historical context, biographical understanding, or external moral reflection to a passage.
