### Stage 1: Pre-existing Decay
Before Common Core was codified, American public education was already severely compromised by the unproven dogmas of constructivist "balanced literacy" (Fountas & Pinnell, Lucy Calkins / Teachers College Reading and Writing Project) and whole-language theory. The system treated reading comprehension as an abstract, transferable skill rather than a function of systematic decoding phonics and cumulative, content-rich background knowledge (E.D. Hirsch Jr.). Furthermore, No Child Left Behind (2001) had already established the managerial pathology that high-stakes psychometric surveillance could substitute for authentic pedagogical craft.

### Stage 2: Technological & Methodological Catalyst
The rapid rollout of Common Core between 2010 and 2014 supercharged this pre-existing rot through:
- **Mechanized Text Complexity Metrics (Lexile / Coh-Metrix):** Algorithmic formulas pushed developmentally inappropriate reading passages downward into K–2 classrooms.
- **PARCC and SBAC Digital Testing Consortia:** Hundreds of millions of dollars were funneled into computerized testing platforms that consumed weeks of instructional time, creating an aggressive feedback loop where schools abandoned broad curricula in favor of decontextualized, "cold-read" test preparation.
- **The Back-Mapping Fallacy:** Linearly decomposing 12th-grade terminal analytical skills down to Kindergarteners, forcing 5-year-olds into abstract print tasks before foundational neurological pathways were established.

### Stage 3: Proactive Negligence & Institutional Cover-Up
When longitudinal empirical data (2015, 2017, 2019, 2022, 2024 NAEP cycles) systematically revealed that reading scores were falling—particularly among Hispanic, Black, and economically disadvantaged cohorts—credentialed education officials, state departments of education, and venture philanthropy organizations executed proactive negligence:
- They refused to repeal the unvalidated instructional frameworks.
- They engaged in cosmetic rebranding (e.g., New Jersey renaming CCSS to NJSLS while keeping 84% identical).
- They actively suppressed or ignored the success of non-Common Core control states (Texas, Florida), maintaining the technocratic pastiche until post-pandemic literacy collapses made denial impossible.
