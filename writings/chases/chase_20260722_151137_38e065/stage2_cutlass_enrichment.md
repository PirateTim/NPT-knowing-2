#### Stage 1: Pre-existing Decay (The Epistemic Surrender)
Long before the advent of modern Large Language Models, the intellectual foundations of data science underwent a systemic erosion. Academic and corporate institutions devalued causal explanation, structural verification, and human-curated ground truth. "Predictive accuracy" on test datasets became the sole metric of success. If a black-box model could predict an outcome, the fact that its internal reasoning was entirely opaque and un-auditable was treated as an acceptable trade-off. This established a professional culture where **provenance was treated as an administrative burden rather than an epistemic necessity.**

#### Stage 2: Technological Catalyst (The Automation of Plausibility)
The introduction of deep learning, transformer architectures, and massive web-scale training datasets acted as an explosive accelerant. These technologies did not create the disregard for ground truth; they scaled it. By training models on the *Poisoned Archive* of the unverified internet, the Statistical Turn was automated. The technology made the pre-existing decay highly visible and acute by generating **Computational Truthiness**—fluent, grammatically flawless text that mimics the structural cadence of authoritative knowledge but is entirely severed from any empirical chain of custody.

#### Stage 3: Proactive Negligence (The Abdication of the Auditor)
The final stage is the active, intentional choice by credentialed actors (executives, editors, lawyers, and educators) to abandon their timeless duties of verification. Rather than using these statistical engines as mere predictive tools, they proactively fire human researchers, data scientists, and subject-matter experts. They treat the statistical averages of an LLM as a cheap substitute for verified institutional knowledge. This is not accidental; it is a deliberate choice to prioritize cost-cutting and cognitive offloading over epistemic integrity, permanently severing the lineage of human knowledge.

### 5. The Warrants and Tensions

The fundamental tension of the Statistical Turn lies in the difference between **correlation** and **comprehension**. 

A statistical model does not "know" that water is wet; it merely knows that the token "wet" has a high probability of appearing near the token "water" in its training corpus. When we mistake this statistical probability for comprehension, we commit a category error. 

The tragedy of modern data science is that it has rebranded this category error as progress. By celebrating the efficiency of statistical prediction, we have normalized the destruction of provenance. We have built an information ecosystem where the cost of generating plausible-sounding noise is zero, while the cost of verifying empirical ground truth remains high. This economic and technical asymmetry is the driving force behind the end of knowing.
