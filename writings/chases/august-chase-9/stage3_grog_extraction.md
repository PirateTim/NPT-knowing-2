## 2. CONCRETE ENTITY, LITERARY & INSTITUTIONAL INVENTORY

### A. Corporate Executives & Technocrats
- **Elon Musk:** CEO of Tesla, SpaceX, xAI. Internalized Asimov (*Foundation*) at age 10; read Douglas Adams at 12–13 in apartheid South Africa. Launched Tesla Roadster into space containing Asimov's *Foundation* and "DON'T PANIC" dashboard sign. Named SpaceX Mars vessel *Heart of Gold* after Adams' fictional ship. Proposes AI-generated "historically accurate" *Odyssey*; targeting 1,000,000 Optimus robots/year.
- **Sam Altman:** CEO of OpenAI. Stated in 2025 that data centers will cover most of Earth's surface ("maybe we put them in space"). Enchanted by Dennis E. Taylor’s *We Are Legion (We Are Bob)* (post-apocalyptic brain uploading). Signed contract for cryonic head preservation and cloud consciousness upload. Author of *The Intelligence Age* (2024).
- **Jeff Bezos:** Founder of Amazon, Blue Origin. Traced space planetary engineering to 1964 (Mariner 4 Mars flyby) and 1990s Mars colonization discourse. Hosted William Shatner on 2021 Blue Origin flight.
- **Mark Zuckerberg:** CEO of Meta. Author of *Open Source AI & the Battle for the Horizon* (August 2026). Advocates open-source AI as moral crusade and job generator while concurrently cutting Meta headcount.
- **Marc Andreessen:** Co-founder of Andreessen Horowitz (a16z). Author of *The Techno-Optimist Manifesto* (2023, ~5,000 words), establishing the modern executive manifesto template.
- **Dario Amodei:** CEO of Anthropic. Author of *Machines of Loving Grace* (2024/2025), presenting benevolent AI stewardship while distancing Anthropic from "doomerism."
- **Peter Thiel & Marvin Minsky:** Historical transhumanist precursors signed up for post-mortem cryonic suspension.
- **Bill Gates:** Co-founder of Microsoft; publicly affirmed in response to "Will we still need humans?" with "Not for most things."

### B. Scholars, Commentators & Primary Witnesses
- **Prof. Rob Lalka (Tulane University):** Business professor diagnosing the "Businessman-Philosopher" persona; identifies that manifesto optimism is strictly prospective ("still yet to be seen").
- **Ted Chiang:** Author and critic; audited Stuart Russell’s misreading of King Midas, demonstrating the parable is about the ruinous nature of greed rather than AI alignment/specification error.
- **Stuart Russell:** AI computer scientist (UC Berkeley); misattributed the King Midas parable to literal instruction misalignment.
- **William Shatner:** Actor; 2021 Blue Origin passenger who experienced profound grief/dread ("felt like a funeral") seeing Earth's fragility, interrupted by Bezos popping champagne.
- **Robert Zubrin:** Aerospace engineer, co-author of *The Case for Mars* (1996), arguing small outposts could be established within a decade without miraculous technology.
- **Kim Stanley Robinson:** Sci-fi author, *Mars* trilogy (1990s terraforming realism).
- **Noah Smith:** Economist questioning the safety of open-sourcing models with potentially catastrophic capabilities.

### C. Literary, Historical & Parable Canon Misreadings
1. **Isaac Asimov (*Foundation* series / *Encyclopedia Galactica*):**  
   - *Technocrat Misreading (Musk):* "Take actions to prolong civilization and reduce dark ages via great men and predictive engineering."  
   - *Textual Provenance / Actual Meaning:* Historical outcomes are driven by collective socio-economic dynamics, not heroic individuals; computer psychohistory is inherently brittle and collapses into autocratic tyranny.
2. **Douglas Adams (*The Hitchhiker's Guide to the Galaxy*):**  
   - *Technocrat Misreading (Musk):* "Phrasing the question is the hard part; the answer is easy." (Magrathean computer Deep Thought / 42).  
   - *Textual Provenance / Actual Meaning:* Satire of British imperialism, corporate absurdity, and unregulated capitalism. Written on a Hermes typewriter with an "END APARTHEID" sticker while Adams observed South African apartheid from London in 1976–1978. Protagonist Zaphod Beeblebrox is a narcissistic puppet president used by corporations (Sirius Cybernetics) to divert public attention from criminal exploitation.
3. **Dennis E. Taylor (*We Are Legion (We Are Bob)*):**  
   - *Technocrat Misreading (Altman):* Blueprint for post-biological transhuman consciousness preservation in synthetic hardware.  
   - *Textual Provenance / Actual Meaning:* Bleak cautionary outcome where human extinction forces uploaded consciousness into state bondage.
4. **E.M. Forster (*The Machine Stops*, 1909):**  
   - *Textual Reality:* Humans confined to solitary pods dependent on an all-encompassing machine until societal collapse occurs.
5. **King Midas Myth:**  
   - *Technocrat Misreading (Russell):* A technical problem of objective specification.  
   - *Textual Reality (Chiang):* Moral parable illustrating that the unchecked pursuit of material wealth destroys everything of genuine value.
6. **Cecil Rhodes (19th-century Imperialist):**  
   - Direct historical lineage cited by Lepore: *"I would annex the planets if I could."*
