Now, the trap has sprung. The platform has unilaterally torn up the foundational contract of the open web—free access in exchange for discovery and traffic. 

The publishers' proposed solution is the `noindex` tag. To understand the sheer, comical futility of this move, we must look at the technical reality: Google does not offer a granular "do not digest but please still index" switch that carries any real weight. If you use `noindex`, you disappear from the AI Overviews, yes—but you also disappear from Google Search entirely. 

This is the **Hostage Dilemma**. You either allow your work to be stolen and repackaged by an LLM that bypasses your site entirely, or you voluntarily erase yourself from the public index.
