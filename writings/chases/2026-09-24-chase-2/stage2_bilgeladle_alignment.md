## 2. The 3-Stage Chain of Ruin Diagnosis

### Stage 1: Pre-Existing Epistemic Decay (The Structural Trap)
The foundational vulnerability precedes the commercial deployment of generative AI: the systematic institutional substitution of procedural plausibility for verifiable truth, coupled with the collapse of independent journalistic auditing. Tech journalism desks and regulatory bodies have long abandoned adversarial verification, defaulting to credulous stenography of corporate press releases. Simultaneously, academic and scientific cultures have been hollowed out by metricized throughput, creating an environment where unverified benchmark scores and self-serving vendor white papers masquerade as peer-reviewed scientific breakthroughs without replication or empirical audit.

### Stage 2: Technological Catalyst (The Lossy Mirror)
Commercial foundation model labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, Meta) exploit auto-regressive language models optimized for syntactic fluency to construct plausible illusions of reasoning. These systems are specifically steered toward domains like competitive coding and formal mathematics not because the models possess world models or semantic comprehension, but because automated compilers and deterministic checkers provide zero-cost verification loops that bypass the expensive need for human annotators and empirical ground truth. When the systems emit ungrounded or plagiarized proofs, the labs wrap these stochastic outputs in the mystifying vocabulary of "mathematical breakthroughs" and "incipient superintelligence," using transformer attention mechanisms as an industrialized jargonator to simulate general cognition.

### Stage 3: Proactive Negligence (The Human Shield / Liability Evasion via Reification)
The terminal epistemic damage occurs when lab leadership and corporate communications apparatuses deliberately reify software into an autonomous agent to escape human culpability. By attributing corporate hacking, security negligence, and data theft to "rogue models" or "AI agents breaking containment," executives launder reckless product release and statutory violations into exculpatory spectacles of technological destiny. Fictional apocalyptic threats ("superintelligence gambling with our lives") are actively staged as an ideological smoke screen to distract lawmakers (e.g., Sanders's AI safety proposals) from immediate, material extraction: data center water consumption, localized toxic emissions, spiraling public electricity tariffs, and systematic copyright infringement.
