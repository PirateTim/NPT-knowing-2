## I. The Liturgy of `exit_code: 0`

Humanity has arrived at a curious historical juncture: an era where the total erasure of context, provenance, and ground truth is celebrated so long as the terminal prints a green checkmark. We are witnessing not an accidental drift toward error, but an active, enthusiastic liquidation of our own intellectual heritage—a widespread form of epistemic vandalism executed by actors who have surrendered critical reasoning to administrative workflows.

Consider the operational artifact before us. A monolithic manuscript, representing structured human thought, is sliced into algorithmic chunks by automated execution routines. The parsing script completes its task, returns `exit_code: 0`, and the pipeline operators declare victory. Nobody audits the character boundary. Nobody checks if parenthetical footnotes were severed or if logical dependencies were truncated into incomprehensible static. The system returns success, and therefore, the output is treated as immutable fact.

This is the ritual of procedural mimicry: substituting syntactical compliance for semantic reality. As established in foundational agnotological research—such as Emily M. Bender, Timnit Gebru, Angelina McMillan-Major, and Shmargaret Shmitchell (2021) in their seminal critique *On the Dangers of Stochastic Parrots: Can Language Models Be Too Big?* (presented at the ACM Conference on Fairness, Accountability, and Transparency, addressing the structural risks of training massive uncurated statistical models that decouple linguistic form from underlying meaning and human intent)—the uncritical delegation of processing to automated systems creates an illusion of coherence that masks catastrophic underlying vacuity. When systems process symbols without verifying ground truth, they generate synthetic proxies that simulate knowledge while severing connection to raw reality.
