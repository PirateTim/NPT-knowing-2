## 4. Epistemic Taxonomy & Fallacy Scan

### A. Active Epistemic Failure Modes
1. **CUTLASS-RULE-018 (Agnotological Narrative Misdirection & The Naive Intent Fallacy):**
   The asset correctly identifies that executive optimism is completely prospective ("still yet to be seen") while immediate layoffs are empirical, but naively frames this as an innocent "specialized game" among elites rather than a deliberate strategy of distraction and regulatory evasion.
2. **CUTLASS-RULE-005 (Access Journalism via Roadmap Reporting):**
   By treating executive manifestos as legitimate roadmaps of "commitments" rather than manufactured public relations noise, the author uncritically incorporates corporate strategy into consumer buying advice.
3. **CUTLASS-RULE-023 (Anti-Financial Motive Fallacy & Shallow Economic Reduction):**
   Reduces deep structural epistemic warfare to simple market differentiation ("positioning Meta against closed approaches") without analyzing the deeper cybernetic ideology or provenance destruction underpinning all four manifestos.

### B. Logical Fallacies & Structural Flaws
- **False Equivalence of Stewardship:** Equates corporate survival strategy with genuine societal stewardship.
- **Evasion of Evidentiary Proof:** Accepts the premise that Altman, Amodei, and Zuckerberg are "thoughtful stewards" without demanding evidentiary proof of their technical claims.
