## Epistemic Classification: Narrative Misdirection

This asset is a clear example of **Narrative Misdirection**. It frames a chronic, systemic issue—the multi-decade corporate trend of dismantling internal training programs and career pipelines—as an acute crisis caused by a novel technology (AI), dubbing it a "'jobpocalypse.'"

This misdirection serves two distinct agnotological functions:

1.  **It obscures historical responsibility:** By blaming AI, the narrative conveniently ignores the long-term corporate policies and management decisions that have been eroding entry-level opportunities for years. The problem is presented as new and technologically determined, rather than the result of a sustained, deliberate disinvestment in workforce development.
2.  **It creates a pretext for regressive solutions:** The manufactured sense of an unprecedented crisis is used to introduce and legitimize radical, regressive labor models. The suggestion of a "return of a kind of indentured labour" is a key piece of evidence. This reframes a historically exploitative practice as a novel solution to a "super modern" problem, laundering a regressive idea into the mainstream discourse on the future of work.

The asset is therefore a significant artifact. It demonstrates how a long-term, systemic failure is laundered through the lens of technological disruption to both evade accountability and create a rhetorical opening for previously unacceptable social and economic policies.
