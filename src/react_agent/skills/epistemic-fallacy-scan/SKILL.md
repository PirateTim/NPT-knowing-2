# SKILL: Epistemic & Logical Fallacy Scan

## Overview
This skill governs the forensic audit of incoming text assets for epistemic collapse, logical fallacies, and the 3-stage Chain of Ruin.

---

## Audit Taxonomy & Failure Modes

### 1. The 3-Stage Chain of Ruin
- **Stage 1: Pre-existing Decay**: Foundational devaluation of experts, expertise, and ground-truth verification prior to/underlying new technology.
- **Stage 2: Technological Catalyst**: Introduction of AI/automation acting as an acute accelerant that exposes and scales pre-existing decay.
- **Stage 3: Proactive Negligence**: Active choice by credentialed actors (executives, managers, leaders) to intentionally abandon verification duties, eliminate human verifiers, and obliterate provenance.

### 2. Classical Logical Fallacies
- **Strawman**: Misrepresenting or exaggerating an opponent's argument to make it easier to attack.
- **False Dilemma**: Presenting two alternative states as the only possibilities when more exist.
- **Ad Hominem**: Attacking the proponent's character or motives rather than addressing the argument.
- **Red Herring**: Introducing an irrelevant topic to divert attention from the core issue.
- **Begging the Question**: Assuming the conclusion in the premise.
- **Appeal to False Authority**: Citing an unqualified or biased authority.
- **Equivocation**: Misleading use of a key term with multiple meanings.

### 3. Epistemic Taxonomy Highlights
- **War on the Witness**: Physical or structural targeting of frontline human verifiers to force reliance on digital proxies.
- **Texture Hacking**: Simulating intellectual rigor through dense legalistic formatting, press releases, or high-volume PDF output while decoupled from ground truth.
- **Self-Licking Ice Cream Cone**: Official citing a media report that originally drew from the official's own unverified leak.
- **Bush Protocol / Translation Engine Laundering**: Pundits imposing coherent intent or polishing raw, nonsensical institutional statements.
- **View from Nowhere**: Abstracting real-world human suffering into high-level procedural metrics from climate-controlled suites.
- **Epistemic Procrastination**: Forming endless committees and preliminary examinations to defer real-world enforcement.
