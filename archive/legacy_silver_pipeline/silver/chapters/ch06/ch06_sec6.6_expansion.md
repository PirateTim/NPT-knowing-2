### Technocratic Counter-Argument 1: "DMCA § 1202 Was Never Intended to Apply to Machine Learning Training Sets"
* *Adversarial Position:* Section 1202 was drafted to prevent digital piracy and circumvention of watermarks, not to penalize the automated ingestion of data for statistical transformation and general intelligence research.
* *Bilgeladle Epistemic Rebuttal:* The text of DMCA § 1202 contains no technology exemption for large-scale matrix multiplication. Scraping pipelines deliberately and systematically strip headers, author credits, and license manifests from texts specifically to prevent provenance tracking and to hide the copyrighted origins of training sets from downstream auditors. The intent to evade attribution and ownership is the statutory trigger. Arguing that scale grants immunity is an explicit appeal to *Amnesty by Scale*.

### Technocratic Counter-Argument 2: "Enforcing Multi-Trillion-Dollar Liabilities Would Stifle National AI Competitiveness"
* *Adversarial Position:* Bankrupting domestic AI leaders over technical copyright claims would surrender technological leadership to foreign adversaries who operate without copyright restrictions.
* *Bilgeladle Epistemic Rebuttal:* This is the classic technocratic hostage posture: "Legalize our piracy, or the state falls behind." Surrendering statutory property rights, provenance, and the human knowledge archive in exchange for synthetic statistical models is not a strategy for national strength—it is the wholesale destruction of the verified human record to insulate corporate oligarchs from the rule of law.
