1.  **Semantic Ablation & Organized Cannibalism:** By prioritizing cognitive ease, systems are compelled to streamline, simplify, and sanitize information, often discarding nuance, context, and verifiable facts. This leads to **Semantic Ablation**, where meaning is stripped away, and **Organized Cannibalism**, where the system consumes its own validated knowledge, replacing it with easily digestible, but empirically empty, substitutes. This isn't just about misinformation; it's about the industrial refinement of disinformation, where the machine becomes a high-fidelity mirror of humanity's worst inclinations.
4.  **The Erasure of the Territory for the Comfort of the Map:** The deliberate purging of inconvenient or "uncomfortable" historical data and dissenting voices (the "Airbrush," **Data Void**, **Poisoned Archive**) creates a sanitized "map" divorced from the messy "territory" of reality. This vector is a suicide pact because it sacrifices the very capacity for discerning truth at the altar of frictionless experience.
