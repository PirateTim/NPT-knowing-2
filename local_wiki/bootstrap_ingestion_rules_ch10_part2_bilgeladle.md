## 2. Extracted Glossary Terms

The following terms have been successfully ingested and updated in the system glossary:

*   **Defective Human Beings**: The corporate-technocratic view of students (articulated by Rex Tillerson) where human interiority, moral frameworks, and historical context are treated as 'defects' that interfere with corporate compatibility and standardized labor.
*   **Four Corners Doctrine**: An educational protocol (derived from New Criticism and institutionalized by David Coleman) that restricts reading comprehension strictly to the text on the page, criminalizing external historical context, author biography, and reader empathy.
*   **70/30 Mandate**: The Common Core requirement that by 12th grade, 70% of a student's reading must consist of 'Informational Text' (such as regulations, manuals, and documents) rather than 'Literary Text' (fiction, poetry, drama), shifting the educational focus from moral imagination to operational compliance.
*   **Human API**: An educational and cognitive state where a student is formatted to process instructions, scan barcodes, and interface with corporate systems without possessing the internal map, moral imagination, or critical capacity to audit reality.
*   **Variance Problem**: The technocratic view of geographic and socioeconomic disparities in education (Zip Code Apartheid) as engineering inefficiencies to be solved through brutal standardization of outputs rather than resource redistribution.
