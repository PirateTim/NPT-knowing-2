## 2. Extracted Glossary Terms

The following terms have been successfully ingested and updated in the system glossary:

*   **Ghost Facts**: Peer-reviewed findings or scientific assertions whose underlying supplemental data, interactive maps, or primary source archives have been lost or 'bricked' due to proprietary software obsolescence or platform optimization, leaving them un-verifiable.
*   **Dark Data**: Information that technically exists on a server but is functionally unfindable or undecipherable because the human experts, curators, or metadata maps required to retrieve and interpret it have been destroyed or dismissed.
*   **Symbolic Annihilation**: The systematic erasure of specific histories, marginalized voices, or non-consensus truths from the machine-readable record to optimize the archive for statistical averages and algorithmic plausibility.
*   **Institutional Lobotomy**: The systematic dismissal of technical experts, curators, and specialists who function as the 'Living Index' and keepers of tacit knowledge for public records, converting verified datasets into inaccessible 'Dark Data.'
*   **Forward Compatibility Crisis**: The systemic failure of digital repositories and academic platforms to maintain the software environments and metadata standards required to access and verify legacy scientific data over time.
