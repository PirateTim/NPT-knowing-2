## 2. Extracted Glossary Terms

The following terms have been successfully ingested and updated in the system glossary:

*   **Verification Layer**: The structural tier of an information platform that validates identity, authority, and provenance, distinguishing verified institutional actors from bad-faith impersonators.
*   **Micro-Reality**: An epistemic state of extreme information fragmentation where the collapse of global search and centralized platforms forces users into isolated, non-indexed digital fiefdoms (such as private Discord servers, Substacks, or group chats), replacing a shared public reality with localized, un-verifiable micro-realities.
*   **Slop**: Low-quality, algorithmically generated digital content that mimics the structural shape of human communication or art but contains zero empirical, semantic, or nutritional value, designed solely to exploit algorithmic engagement metrics.
*   **Zombie Scroll**: A closed loop of synthetic online activity where automated scripts generate low-quality content (slop) and automated bot networks engage with it to build account history or game algorithms, completely bypassing human participation while simulating an active culture.
*   **Enshittification**: The systematic degradation of a digital platform's utility and user experience by its corporate owners to extract maximum financial value, transforming once-reliable public utilities into monetized, SEO-saturated wastelands.
*   **Zizek Loop**: A recursive loop of automated information processing where generative AI models write content to feed schedules, and human users employ AI summarization tools to read and condense that content, resulting in thoughts that are processed but never actually comprehended by a human mind.
*   **The Matmos**: A living, churning digital slurry of AI-generated content, deepfakes, and SEO-bait that feeds on the excess energy of human attention, weaponized by algorithms to induce fatal levels of engagement and cognitive passivity.
