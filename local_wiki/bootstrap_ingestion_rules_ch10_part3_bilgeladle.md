## 2. Extracted Glossary Terms

The following terms have been successfully ingested and updated in the system glossary:

*   **Syntax Delusion**: The flawed educational belief that students can read and comprehend complex texts without explicit instruction in syntax, sentence diagramming, and structural language engineering.
*   **Boolean Blindspot**: The cognitive vulnerability resulting from the removal of formal logic and truth tables from curricula, leaving individuals unable to distinguish between valid arguments and persuasive lies or to identify logical fallacies (like the converse error).
*   **Surface Skimmers**: Students who can decode text and follow basic instructions but cannot sustain a coherent argument, analyze complex narratives, or retain deep knowledge because they lack structural schema.
*   **Warrantless Processing**: A pattern-matching cognitive state where individuals link claims to evidence without understanding the underlying logical warrants or principles of truth, functioning as a human middleware layer.
*   **Interface Native**: A generation of users who are highly fluent in navigating digital interfaces (swiping, clicking, scrolling) but completely illiterate in the underlying code, logic, and recursive algorithms that govern those systems.
