## 2. Extracted Glossary Terms

The following terms have been successfully ingested and updated in the system glossary:

*   **Identity Stripping**: The process of extracting content (the 'Stone') while discarding the author's context, metadata, and human lineage (the 'Wall'), reducing a structured work to an anonymous, commodified data point.
*   **Ontological Smoking Gun**: The logical contradiction where technology companies classify the scraping of human-created data as fair-use 'learning' while simultaneously classifying the scraping or distillation of their own models as 'theft' or a 'cyberattack.'
*   **Amnesty by Scale**: The structural phenomenon where a corporate or technical liability is so massive and deeply entangled with the economy that enforcing the law becomes a systemic risk, resulting in de facto legal immunity for the violators.
*   **Vegetal Memory**: The physical, stubborn, and verified preservation of human knowledge on paper or physical media, which resists centralized deletion and digital alteration, contrasted with fluid and mutable silicon-based memory.
*   **Mineral Memory**: The fluid, mutable, and easily altered preservation of knowledge on silicon-based digital media, which is highly vulnerable to centralized deletion, licensing traps, and platform purges.
*   **Generation Loss**: The progressive degradation of factual accuracy, context, and provenance as information is repeatedly processed, summarized, or generated by AI models without reference to its original source or metadata.
*   **Vaporization Event**: The sudden, complete deletion of digital archives, data, or history by corporate or state actors, leaving only empty metadata or broken links.
*   **Jurisdictional Exposure**: The vulnerability of digital data to national laws and executive actions based on the physical location of the hosting servers, regardless of the owner's international status or location.
