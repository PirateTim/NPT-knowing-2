## 2. Extracted Glossary Terms

The following terms have been successfully ingested and updated in the system glossary:

*   **Tortured Phrases**: Nonsensical linguistic artifacts generated by automated translation or AI paraphrasing tools designed to bypass plagiarism detectors, which are then published in peer-reviewed journals due to automated workflow optimization.
*   **Welded Tire**: The systemic problem in generative AI where fraudulent, retracted, or p-hacked data is permanently baked into a model's weights during training, making it impossible to extract or correct without computationally prohibitive re-training.
*   **Hollow Library**: An epistemic state where academic papers and scientific claims (the PDFs) persist, but the underlying supplemental data, proofs, and hyperlinks (the receipts) have rotted, broken, or been deleted, rendering the claims unverifiable.
*   **Velocity of Rot**: The rapid rate at which digital hyperlinks, supplemental materials, and data repositories associated with scientific publications break or disappear, rendering a significant portion of the scientific record unverifiable within years of publication.
*   **Epistemic Procrastination**: The institutional failure of learned societies and academic bodies to establish and enforce standards for digital persistence, focusing instead on citation aesthetics or format legitimization while ignoring the fragility of digital evidence.
