### 2. Epistemic Vandalism & Intentional Provenance Obliteration

The core epistemic vandalism in this asset is twofold:

1.  **The Elimination of Specialized Verification Roles**: By replacing specialized data scientists and researchers with "generalist engineers," Instagram is structurally eliminating the very roles responsible for establishing ground truth, auditing algorithmic bias, and verifying platform safety. Generalists are incentivized to ship code quickly, not to audit its societal or epistemic consequences.

This is a classic corporate maneuver to launder labor degradation and the loss of oversight capacity into a narrative of "efficiency" and "streamlining."

The underlying warrant of Mosseri's argument is that **speed and cost-reduction are superior to empirical verification and platform safety**. Specialized researchers and data scientists, who do the slow, difficult work of verification, are treated as friction to be eliminated.

The tension here is between the corporate desire for rapid, un-audited deployment of AI systems and the societal need for platform safety and empirical truth. By choosing to "move faster" with fewer specialists, Instagram is actively choosing to operate in an epistemic vacuum, relying on "computational truthiness" and algorithmic prediction rather than empirical reality.
