1. (1) Investigate the foundational pedagogical theory, philosophy of education, and instructional assumptions underlying the Common Core standards in literacy and mathematics. 

2. (2) Analyze the origins, historical timeline, and philanthropic/federal funding mechanisms (e.g., Gates Foundation, Race to the Top) to assess whether funding preceded the pedagogy or if pedagogical ideas earned the funding. 

3. (3) Trace the history of Common Core adoption, standardized testing, and subsequent revisions in New Jersey, specifically examining the roles, initiatives, and policies of Chris Christie and Cory Booker and Governor Sherrill. Compare NJ to other states in both process and outcomes.

4. (4) Search for executive actions, legislative records, and public statements by Governor Sherrill regarding New Jersey educational standards, curriculum mandates, and literacy policies to determine the factors driving those measures. 

5. (5) Identify and review reports and standardized assessment data released in September 2026 regarding trends and steep declines in reading and literacy scores nationally and in New Jersey. 

6. (6) Gather empirical research and longitudinal academic studies evaluating the measurable impact of Common Core implementation on student achievement over the past decade. 

7. (7) Assess the credibility and reliability of the gathered sources, contrasting peer-reviewed research, official state education department data, and think tank or interest group analyses. 

8. (8) Synthesize the findings into an extensive four-chapter research report emphasizing pedagogical foundations, funding chronology, New Jersey legislative history, decade-long empirical outcomes, and recent reading score trends.
