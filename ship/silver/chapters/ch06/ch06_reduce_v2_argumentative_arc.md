### Section 6.1: The Diver and the Net — The Embodied Epistemology of Ground Truth
* **Narrative Opening:** In the freezing waters of a Norwegian fjord, Bryton Shang (Aquabyte) discovers that automated fish farming cannot operate on disembodied statistical proxies; optical sensors must calibrate against the physical biomass, sea-lice counts, and hydrodynamic realities of living organisms.
* **Philosophical Premise:** True intelligence requires direct sensorimotor or empirical coupling with reality. Knowledge is an ongoing physical calibration against friction, not a closed pattern-matching exercise across disembodied symbols.
* **Toulmin Warrant:** Without a physical or biological ground-truth anchor, automated systems do not perceive reality; they merely calculate the self-consistent geometry of their own sensors.
* **Dialectical Function:** Establishes the Chapter's ontological standard—real knowledge is expensive, embodied, and grounded in physical friction.

* **Philosophical Premise:** High-quality language is not an infinite renewable resource; it is the finite biological deposit of centuries of human intellectual labor.
* **Toulmin Warrant:** Scaling laws ($L \approx C^\alpha D^\beta$) require exponential growth in clean training tokens ($D$) to sustain linear improvements in loss; when $D$ peaks, the scaling hypothesis structurally terminates.
