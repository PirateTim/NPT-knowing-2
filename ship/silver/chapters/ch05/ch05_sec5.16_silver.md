#### 5.16 The Breach (The "For You" Poison)
The victory was temporary. The company, desperate for growth, eventually forced the "For You" feed as the default. This was the breach that flooded the P4 Lab. The algorithm’s directive was to maximize engagement, and it understood that the most efficient fuel for engagement is Context Collapse. It took a tweet meant for a specific Trust Graph—say, a nuanced debate between two progressive housing policy experts—and injected it into the feed of a hostile "Outgroup" (e.g., suburban Nimbys or alt-right trolls).

The result wasn't a "debate"; it was a riot. The "For You" feed replaced Authority (The Expert Amateur) with Punditry (The Rage Merchant). The "Cost of Speech" for an expert became too high. If a virologist posted a study, they weren't peer-reviewed by other scientists; they were swarmed by thousands of anonymous accounts screaming about microchips. The algorithm proved that if you force everyone to share the same reality, you degrade the reality for everyone.

The Right Wing, feeling persecuted by the moderation that still existed, tried to build their own Arks. They launched Parler, Gettr, and eventually Donald Trump’s Truth Social. They all failed, and they failed for an epistemological reason: Conflict is the Product. These platforms became echo chambers populated by what the internet politely calls "The Freaks".
