### The Syntax of Nothing

I saw the warning signs long before ChatGPT. I saw it back in the early 2000s, watching George W. Bush speak.

I used to tell my colleagues that **Bush was the only person on earth who didn't have a first language.** He didn't actually make sentences. He didn't rely on subjects, verbs, and objects. He often didn't even use real words.

He was the human prototype for the Large Language Model. He would emit a stream of "patriotic tokens"—*families, working, hard, terror, freedom*—and rely on the audience to assemble them into a thought. He proved that if you generate the right sounds from behind the right podium, the "Parser" (the public) will fill in the gaps for you.

In 2026, that "Bush Logic" is the operating system of the planet.

We have not just entered an era of "misinformation." That implies we have good data mixed with bad.
We have entered the **End of Knowing**.

This is not a "Garbage In, Garbage Out" problem. That is a cliché for junior developers.
This is a **Schema Failure**.

We have dismantled the only mechanism we had for turning a "Data Point" into "Knowledge": **Verification.**
* We destroyed the **Provenance** (Where did this come from?).
* We destroyed the **Context** (When was this written?).
* We destroyed the **Author** (Who stands behind this?).
