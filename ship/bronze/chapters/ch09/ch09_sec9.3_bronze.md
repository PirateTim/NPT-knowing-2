#### 9.3 The Welded Tire and the Retraction Crisis

This was not a routine administrative reorganization; it was an admission of total systemic failure. Wiley reported the retraction of over 11,300 papers that had been identified as products of "systematic manipulation" by AI-driven paper mills (Wiley/ISSN, 2024). We know the scale of this disaster only because the volume of "Tortured Phrases" and fabricated data became so volumetric that it threatened the brand equity of the publisher itself. The "Plausibility-Based Gates" of the 2000s—the Aries and ScholarOne systems we discussed in Section II—had finally been overwhelmed by the very "Efficiency" they were designed to facilitate.

However, the retraction of 11,300 papers is a forensic illusion. In the era of the "Mineral Memory," a retraction is a "Ghost Fact"—an institutional footnote that holds no power over the mathematical reality of the machine. This is the "Welded Tire" problem of the 2026 knowledge system. When a Large Language Model like GPT-4 or Claude is trained, it ingests the entire "Clean Data" hoard of the pre-2023 web, including the thousands of fraudulent papers that passed through the Big Five's optimized pipelines before the "Data Detectives" like Guillaume Cabanac could sound the alarm. Once that data is "baked" into the weights of a model, it cannot be easily extracted. It is not a file that can be deleted; it is a statistical pattern that has been welded into the machine's very conception of "Ground Truth."

The technical reality of "catastrophic forgetting" ensures that you cannot simply tell a Trillion-Token model to "forget" the fraudulent hydrology tables of a Hindawi paper without compromising the model’s entire understanding of hydrology. To purge the 11,300 retractions would require re-training the model from scratch—an endeavor that, in the context of the $100 billion "Stargate" projects, is economically and computationally impossible. Consequently, we have built a civilizational "Assistant" that is learning its science from the very fraud that the human "Immune System" has already identified and rejected. The AI is not merely hallucinating; it is reflecting the "Industrialized Simulacrum" that was enclosed and optimized by the Big Five during the decades of "Metadata Compliance."

The forensic implications for the "End of Knowing" are profound. If the training set includes thousands of "p-hacked" or paper-mill-generated studies that have since been retracted, the model will continue to replicate those findings as if they were verifiable facts.
