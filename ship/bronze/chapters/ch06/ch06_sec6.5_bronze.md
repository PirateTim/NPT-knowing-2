### 6.5 The Broken Metaphor
The legal defense for this arson relies on a single metaphor: "The AI is a Student." The tech companies argue that training a model is legally identical to a human student reading in a library.

In February 2026, Google itself destroyed this metaphor. In their *Threat Horizons* report, Google’s security analysts classified "Model Distillation"—where a competitor queries a model millions of times to extract its weights—as a "Cyberattack" and "IP Theft". 



This is the **Ontological Smoking Gun**. 
* When Google scrapes the human record, they call it **"Learning"**. 
* When a competitor scrapes Google’s model, they call it **"Theft"**. 

By classifying "high-volume reading" of their own model as a crime, Google admitted that the machine is not a student. It is a database. If "Distillation" is theft, then "Training" is a heist.
