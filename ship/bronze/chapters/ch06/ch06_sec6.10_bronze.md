### 6.10 The Five Families: Infrastructure of Persistence
Between 1990 and 2010, the global community constructed DOIs and CLOCKSS to ensure knowledge remained verifiable. A DOI (Digital Object Identifier) identifies the object itself, ensuring the Stones remained fixed in the Wall even as corporate ground shifted.

The AI industry represents a bypass of this infrastructure. Datasets like **C4** and **The Pile** were built to ignore the DOI. By violating the metadata governing Open Access, architects performed a mass "Identity Stripping" of the scientific record. This results in **Generation Loss**: a fact becomes a "Plausible Pattern" with no link to its witness.
