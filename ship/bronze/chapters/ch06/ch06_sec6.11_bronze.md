### 6.11 The Metadata Suicide
Taylor Swift’s re-recording project, while framed as artist rights, serves as a metadata hack—a bifurcation of the record. By creating "Two Sons" with the same name, the project forces a choice between the **Historical Stone** (the original recording) and the **Shadow Stone** (the re-recording). When a database encounters identical titles with different metadata fingerprints, it undergoes an identity crisis. This impulse has a legacy of wreckage, from Prince’s abandonment of his name for a symbol to Tony Orlando’s royalty reclaim. It is a "Neighborhood of Nowhere" where the public can no longer distinguish the Stone from the Ghost.
