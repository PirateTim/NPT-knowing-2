### 6.13 Case Study: The Sovereign Cloud (ICC vs. Microsoft)
The termination of the ICC’s access to its evidence in 2025 exposed the flaw of **Jurisdictional Exposure**. The ICC believed their data was immune because they were in The Hague, but their data was on Microsoft Azure. When the U.S. signed **Executive Order 14203** sanctioning the ICC Chief Prosecutor, Microsoft complied. The Chief Prosecutor was disconnected from his own evidence. In the **Hard 2025**, there is no "International Law" on "National Infrastructure". If the server is American, the evidence is American.
