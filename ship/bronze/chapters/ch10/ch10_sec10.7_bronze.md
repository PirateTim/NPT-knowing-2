### 10.7 The Whistleblowers: The Refusal to Sign

We cannot accept the defense that the designers of the Common Core were merely "misguided" or "naive" about the consequences of removing these tools. We know they were warned, because the experts they hired to validate the system refused to sign their names to the death warrant.

The **Common Core Validation Committee** was established to provide a veneer of academic rigor to the project. However, the two subject-matter experts with the highest credentials—**Dr. Sandra Stotsky** (English Language Arts) and **Dr. James Milgram** (Mathematics)—refused to certify the standards.

Dr. Stotsky, the architect of the highly successful Massachusetts standards, identified the "Four Corners" doctrine and the removal of syntax as a mechanism for cognitive atrophy. She warned that by replacing complex literature with "informational texts" and removing the requirement for historical context, the standards would produce **"empty skill sets"** rather than literate citizens (Stotsky, 2010). She explicitly predicted the creation of a generation of **"Surface Skimmers"**—students who could decode a user manual but could not sustain a coherent argument because they lacked the "Schema" to hold it together.

Dr. Milgram was equally scathing regarding the mathematics standards. He warned that by removing the "Logic Strand" and capping the curriculum at Algebra II, the Common Core was essentially legislating a **"mathematical permanent underclass"** (Milgram, 2010). He explicitly stated that the standards were **"not comparable"** to those of high-performing nations and would leave American students unprepared for university-level STEM fields.

When Stotsky and Milgram refused to sign, the system did not pause to reflect. It purged them. The validation report was published with their names listed as "non-signatories," and the machine rolled forward. This proves the intentionality of the lobotomy. The system didn't accidentally forget to include logic and literature; it actively overruled the experts who told them it was necessary.
