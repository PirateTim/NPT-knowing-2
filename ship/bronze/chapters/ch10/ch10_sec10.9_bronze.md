### 10.9 The Interface Native: The Coding Gap

The consequences of this "Syntax Delusion" extend far beyond the English classroom. In the 21st century, the ability to structure thought is the ability to write code.

There is a pervasive myth that "Coding is Math." This is false. A 2020 study by **Prat et al.** published in *Scientific Reports* found that the strongest predictor of Python programming ability was not mathematical aptitude, but **Language Aptitude** (Prat et al., 2020).

To code, you must understand **Recursion**, **Nesting**, and **Conditional Dependency**. You must understand that an `IF/THEN` statement is structurally identical to a complex sentence with a dependent clause.

By removing the **Sentence Diagram** and the **Truth Table**, we lobotomized the "Digital Native." We created a generation that is fluent in the **Interface** (swipe, click, scroll) but illiterate in the **Code**. Because they cannot parse recursive sentences, they struggle to parse recursive algorithms. They are "Interface Natives," passing through a digital world they are structurally incapable of auditing.

This is the "Skill Illusion." We told them they were learning "21st Century Skills" while stripping them of the 19th Century tools required to master the 21st Century machine.
