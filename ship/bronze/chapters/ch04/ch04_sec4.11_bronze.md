#### 4.11 The Lizard Brain as Mastermind
When Donald Trump arrived in 2016, the media tried to apply the "Bush Protocol" to a new kind of incoherence. But Trump wasn't stumbling over syntax; he was operating on a frequency of pure, fractal chaos. Yet, the media persisted. They could not accept that the President was a chaotic actor, because that would reduce them to the role of babysitters cleaning up a mess. To maintain their own status as "Essential Workers," they needed him to be a Strategic Super-Villain.

When Trump tweeted "Covfefe" or attacked the NFL at 3 a.m., pundits like Chris Cillizza and Maggie Haberman did not report it as "Impulse Control Disorder." They reported it as "4D Chess" (Cillizza, 2017). They wrote thousands of words analyzing his "Distraction Strategy," arguing that he was creating a spectacle to divert attention from a bad news cycle (Stelter, 2020). This was the ultimate act of Professional Narcissism. They elevated his incompetence to "Grand Strategy" because it elevated them to "Grand Inquisitors." They became the Destroyers, looting the public’s perception of reality to inflate their own sense of purpose.
