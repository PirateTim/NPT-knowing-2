#### 4.14 The Suicide of the Scribe
If the "Translation Engine" was the mechanism by which the media normalized the incoherence of the President, "Copaganda" is the mechanism by which they launder the violence of the State. It is here, on the police beat, that the "Militant Laziness" of the profession curdles into something far more toxic: Ritualized Collaboration. The structural definition of "News" in 2025 is simply: What the police say happened.

This is not a conspiracy; it is a curriculum. Journalism schools have spent decades teaching a generation of reporters that "Objectivity" is defined as Accurate Attribution rather than Empirical Verification. The young reporter is taught that if they accurately transcribe what the Police Sergeant said, they have done their job. If the Sergeant lied, that is not the reporter’s problem; it is the public’s problem. This pedagogical failure created a suicide pact. By accepting "The Official Statement" as a substitute for "The Truth," the reporter absolves themselves of the duty to witness. They become stenographers with a press pass (U.S. Press Freedom Tracker, 2020). And when the public—armed with the ubiquitous panopticon of the smartphone—does witness the truth, the reporter is not just exposed as incompetent; they are exposed as a liar.
