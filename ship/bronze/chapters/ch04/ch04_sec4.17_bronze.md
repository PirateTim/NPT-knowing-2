#### 4.17 The Expert Laundromat
When the police narrative is challenged—when the video leaks or the family screams too loudly—the media engages a secondary defense mechanism: The Expert Laundromat. They call in a "Use of Force Expert" or a "Security Consultant." Invariably, this expert is a retired police officer (Balko, 2013). The reporter feels "unsafe" making a moral judgment about the violence, so they outsource the judgment to a member of the same fraternity that committed the violence. The result is a technocratic defense that launders the violence through a third party. It allows the reporter to stand behind the shield of "Both Sides"—The family says it was murder; the Expert says it was protocol—without ever having to acknowledge that the "Protocol" itself might be monstrous.

This extends to the Macro Narrative. We see it in the "Retail Theft Epidemic" stories of 2023, where outlets printed National Retail Federation data claiming a massive spike in organized shoplifting. The "News" terrified the suburbs with tales of lawless gangs stripping shelves. Months later, when the actual FBI data and earnings calls were analyzed, the "spike" was revealed to be a fabrication—often covering up corporate mismanagement or self-checkout errors (Los Angeles Times, 2023). But the retraction was printed on page A20; the terror was printed on A1.
