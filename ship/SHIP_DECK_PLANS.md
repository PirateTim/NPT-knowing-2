# The End of Knowing: Ship Deck Plans (Master Chapter Backbones & Epistemic Ontology)

This document establishes the canonical structural blueprint ("Ship Deck Plans") of *The End of Knowing*. It defines the diagnostic coordinate system used by **BILGELADLE** and the NPT Fleet to categorize, evaluate, and anchor all external cargo assets to the manuscript thesis.

---

## 1. The Architectural Schema

Every chapter in *The End of Knowing* is structured around a **5-Part Macro-Backbone**, and each section functions as an **Individual Rib** carrying operational metadata.

### A. The 5-Part Chapter Backbone (Macro-Vector)
1. **Chapter Identifier & Title**: The structural division of the manuscript.
2. **Subject Sector**: The specific institutional, professional, or commercial domain responsible for the epistemic assault.
3. **Vector of Destruction**: The overarching mechanism by which that sector systematically destroys information provenance or verification.
4. **Immutable Predicates**: The two invariant verbs of civilizational damage:
   * `destroys our ability to know anything` *(The Knower: Immediate cognitive blinding and radical skepticism inflicted on the citizen)*
   * `does permanent epistemic damage to the human knowledge system` *(The Archive: Irreversible contamination and erasure of the civilizational record)*
5. **Universal Doom Point**: The catastrophic terminal failure state where truth is liquidated to preserve institutional, commercial, or state power.

### B. The 7-Part Section Rib (Operational Metadata)
1. **Section ID & Title**: The granular manuscript section (e.g. `4.26`, `6.6`).
2. **Operative Actor & Role**: The bylined individual, practitioner, or political operative making the immediate choice to dissociate from obtainable empirical facts.
3. **Upstream Enabler**: The institutional gatekeeper, masthead, or publisher granting the authoritative platform or imprimatur.
4. **Behavioral Qualifier**: The specific deliberate choice, starting with `"by choosing to..."` or `"by choosing not to obtain obtainable empirical facts..."`.
5. **Chain of Ruin Stage**:
   * *Stage 1: Pre-Existing Decay* (Institutional, cultural, or ethical rot preceding technology)
   * *Stage 2: Technological Catalyst* (Automation, scraping, metrics, or LLMs scaling the decay)
   * *Stage 3: Proactive Negligence* (Deliberate abandonment of verification duty and assertion of impunity)
6. **Specific Doom Point**: The local terminal catastrophe resulting from this specific choice.
7. **Witness Warrants**: Historical citations, whistleblowers, and empirical studies diagnosing the pathology (strictly quarantined; never subjects of destruction triples).
