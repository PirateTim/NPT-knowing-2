### Premise 1: Tacit Knowledge is Non-Algorithmic and Irreplaceable by Data Pipelines
* **Manuscript Basis:** Paul Yura’s departure as Warning Coordination Meteorologist (WCM) carried away three decades of localized hydrology context: the "pulse" of the Guadalupe River, the exact geographic boundaries of the camp's cellular dead zone, and the operational rule that an automated alert is meaningless unless confirmed via landline.

Data is an abstract measurement; *knowing* is the operational synthesis of data, physical constraints, human behavior, and local history. * **Manuscript Basis:** Bryton Shang, acting as a DOGE advisor for "Workforce Optimization," flagged the WCM position as "Non-Essential / Replaceable by Automated Dissemination."

In Shang’s metric space, paying a salary to a human to pick up a telephone was an "antiquated pipeline of human redundancy." He substituted the *texture of automation* (API endpoints, instant notifications) for the *semantic reality of communication* (ensuring the human recipient actually heard and understood the message).

* **Manuscript Basis:** Kerr County rejected a $1 million upstream flood gauge proposal over a $950,000 loan burden, only to approve a $45,000 LoRaWAN mesh network system three months *after* twenty-eight people drowned.

They prefer to rely on centralized, software-based "solutions" that appear free or low-cost on paper. The post-disaster installation of the $45,000 LoRaWAN network proves that the technical solution was trivial; what was lacking was the institutional valuation of physical ground truth over financial optics.

* **Manuscript Basis:** The text cites Rand’s *Atlas Shrugged*: *"The man who attempts to purchase the brains of his superiors to serve him, with his money replacing his judgment, ends up by becoming the victim of his inferiors."*
