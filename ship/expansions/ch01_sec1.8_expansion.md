### Premise A: The Chomskyan Diagnosis — Syntax Without Explanation

An autoregressive LLM, constrained only by statistical co-occurrence in its training corpus, cannot distinguish between a physically bound truth and a plausible-sounding impossibility.

### Premise B: The LeCun Surrender — The Autoregressive Dead-End
> *"Auto-Regressive LLMs are doomed... on the highway toward human-level AI, a large language model is basically an off-ramp — a distraction, a dead end."*  
> — Yann LeCun (May 22, 2024)

* **Forensic Breakdown:** As the champion of Connectionism, LeCun’s agreement with Chomsky’s diagnosis represents a decisive structural admission. LeCun identified that predicting the next token ($y_{t+1}$ given $y_1, y_2, \dots, y_t$) creates an exponential error accumulation trajectory.

Yet, despite this astronomical data ingestion, these models fail at basic physical manipulation tasks (such as predicting how objects tumble or how a dinner table is cleared) that a four-year-old child masters through minimal physical interaction.
* **Statistical Attraction vs. Physical Causality:** An LLM associates "dropped glass" with "shatter" because those tokens frequently co-occur in text. However, the model possesses no internal representation of kinetic energy, fragile lattice structures, or gravitational acceleration. It knows the *words*, but not the *world*.

> *"The 'Camp Mystic' lie was the perfect child of this convergence: it satisfied Chomsky’s rules of grammar (it sounded like a news report) and LeCun’s rules of probability (it predicted the tokens for 'rescue'), but it failed the only rule that matters—physical reality."*
