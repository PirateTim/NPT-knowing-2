That was the "News." It was a lie. The "Truth" was captured by Darnella Frazier, a 17-year-old girl with a smartphone. She did not have a journalism degree. She did not have a "Press Pass" issued by the Deputy Commissioner. She simply had the courage to witness.

The contrast between the Star Tribune headline ("Medical Incident") and the Frazier video (Murder) was the Humiliation of the Industry. It proved that "Credentials" were now inversely correlated with "Truth." The audience realized that the only honest reporting was coming from outside the payroll. The "News" had become a lagging indicator of reality, waiting for permission to speak, while the "Feed" was the leading indicator, streaming the raw, bloody data of the street.
