#### 4.10 Patient Zero: The Aluminum Tubes
Once the Translation Engine was running, it was only a matter of time before a sophisticated actor hijacked it. That actor was Dick Cheney, and his accomplice was Judith Miller. In the lead-up to the Iraq War, Cheney did not need to "translate" incoherence; he needed to "inject" malice. He exploited the media’s Sourcing Fetish—the belief that a lie told by an anonymous official is more valuable than a truth told by a dissident. As I.F. Stone warned decades earlier, "All governments lie, but disaster lies in wait for countries whose officials smoke the same hashish they give out" [I. F. Stone (1967), "In a Time of Torment: 1961–1967", Random House, URL: https://archive.org/details/intimeoftorment00ston].

The mechanism was the Self-Licking Ice Cream Cone. First, the Injection: The White House leaked the false intelligence about aluminum tubes to Judith Miller at The New York Times [J. Miller & M. R. Gordon (2002), "U.S. says Hussein intensifies quest for A-bomb parts", The New York Times, URL: https://www.nytimes.com/2002/09/08/world/threats-responses-iraqis-us-says-hussein-intensifies-quest-for-bomb-parts.html]. Second, the Laundering: Miller published the story on September 8, 2002, citing "senior administration officials." The lie now possessed the texture of the "Paper of Record." Third, the Validation: Hours later, Cheney appeared on Meet the Press and cited the Times article as independent corroboration. "It’s not just us saying it," he effectively argued. "Look at the Times" [NBC News (2002), "Meet the Press: Vice President Dick Cheney discusses Iraqi Nuclear Threat and Aluminum Tubes", NBC News Transcript, URL: https://www.nbcnews.com/id/wbna3080244].

Judith Miller was not a victim in this loop. She was not a gullible reporter tricked by a master spy. She was the Scorpion. She knew the game. As Michael Hastings later diagnosed in The Operators, "The price of being in the room is that you don't report the truth" [Michael Hastings (2012), "The Operators: The Wild and Terrifying Inside Story of America's War in Afghanistan", Blue Rider Press / Penguin Group, URL: https://archive.org/details/the-operators-michael-hastings]. She traded her credibility for the "Scoop of the Century" because the incentives of the profession demanded it. She chose to be a Player in the war rather than a Referee of the facts.
