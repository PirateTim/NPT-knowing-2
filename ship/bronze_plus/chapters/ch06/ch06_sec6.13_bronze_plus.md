### 6.13 Case Study: The Sovereign Cloud (ICC vs. Microsoft)
The termination of the ICC’s access to its evidence in 2025 exposed the flaw of **Jurisdictional Exposure**. The ICC believed their data was immune because they were in The Hague, but their data was on Microsoft Azure. When the U.S. signed **Executive Order 14203** sanctioning the ICC Chief Prosecutor [Donald J. Trump (2025), "Executive Order 14203: Imposing Sanctions on the International Criminal Court", Executive Office of the President / Federal Register (90 FR 8451), URL: https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/02/11/2025-02636/imposing-sanctions-on-the-international-criminal-court], Microsoft complied. The Chief Prosecutor was disconnected from his own evidence [Richard Speed (2025), "International Criminal Court kicks Microsoft Office to the curb: ZenDiS openDesk to replace proprietary software", The Register, URL: https://www.theregister.com/2025/10/31/icc_dumps_microsoft_for_opendesk/]. In the **Hard 2025**, there is no "International Law" on "National Infrastructure". If the server is American, the evidence is American.
