### 6.6 The Load-Bearing Crime (The Two Pams)
If the metaphor is broken, why has the law failed? The answer is structural **Amnesty by Scale**. In 2026, the public was presented with the two faces of the **Impunity Doctrine**.

**The Folklore of Collapse:** Following the redaction of the Epstein files, a viral rumor claimed Attorney General Pam Bondi warned that if the full list were released, "the system will collapse". She never said it; the quote was a fabrication [Snopes Fact Check Staff (2026), "Fact Check: Pam Bondi Statements, Legal Claims, and Misinformation", Snopes, URL: https://www.snopes.com/fact-check/pam-bondi-constitution/]. But the lie traveled at the speed of light because it accurately described the architecture: the crimes were so entangled with the state that justice had become a structural risk.

**The Math of Ruin:** Pamela Samuelson [Pamela Samuelson (2025), "Will Copyright Derail Generative AI Technologies?", Schwartz Reisman Institute / UC Berkeley School of Law, URL: https://sri.utoronto.ca/events/pamela-samuelson-will-copyright-derail-generative-ai-technologies] provided the arithmetic. She pointed to Section 1202 of the DMCA, which imposes a $\$2,500$ fine for removing "Copyright Management Information" (CMI). Multiplied by the $5$ billion works ingested by AI models, the total is **$\$12.5$ Trillion**. This is not a fine; it is an extinction event exceeding the combined market cap of the companies involved. The liability has achieved escape velocity from the law. The theft is now load-bearing.
