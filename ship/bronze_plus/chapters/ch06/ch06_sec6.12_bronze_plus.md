### 6.12 The Great Vanishing: Platform Purges
The terminal state is the **Vaporization Event**, where Metadata remains but Data vanishes. The state performed a mass vaporization when the Secret Service deleted text messages surrounding the January 6 Capitol breach under the guise of "device migration" [Department of Homeland Security Office of Inspector General (2022), "The Secret Service’s Deletion of Text Messages Related to January 6, 2021", DHS OIG Congressional Notification, URL: https://www.oig.dhs.gov/sites/default/files/assets/2022-07/Congressional-Notification-USSS-Texts.pdf]. In the private sector, Joe Ricketts shuttered *DNAinfo* and *Gothamist* in 2017 [Oliver Darcy (2017), "Joe Ricketts Shuts Down DNAinfo and Gothamist", CNNMoney, URL: https://money.cnn.com/2017/11/02/media/dnainfo-gothamist-shutting-down/index.html], vaporizing a decade of investigative journalism in a single afternoon because the utility of breaking a union outweighed the science of the public record.
