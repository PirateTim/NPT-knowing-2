### 10.13 The Terminal Event: The Hanover Hallucination

The "End of Knowing" is not theoretical. It is operational. We can see the final product of this system in the actions of **Gregory Skiff**, a Board of Education member in **Hanover Township, New Jersey**, in January 2026 [Hanover Township Board of Education (2026), "Minutes of the January Meeting regarding the Red Carpet Inn Proposal", Hanover Township Public Schools, URL: https://www.hanovertwpschools.com/our-district/board-of-education].

Skiff is not a victim of the system; he is its avatar. A young, high-functioning bureaucrat raised in the "Skills-Based" era, Skiff proposed turning the local **Red Carpet Inn**—a dilapidated, crime-ridden motel adjacent to a chaotic highway—into a federal detention facility.

To the "Old Mind" (the Citizen), this idea is insane. It violates the physical reality of the town, the safety of the children, and the moral logic of community. But Skiff does not operate in physical reality. He operates in **Textual Reality**.

Skiff looked at the proposal and saw that it met the *criteria* of the text. It aligned with federal policy codes. It generated revenue numbers that balanced a spreadsheet. He possessed the "skill" of argumentation—he could cite the statutes—but he lacked the **Schema** of the world. He shares the destroyer's philosophy: that if the "Evidence" fits the "Claim," the reality is irrelevant.

Skiff is the "Defective Product" Rex Tillerson ordered. He is a leader who can process the prompt but cannot see the fire. He is the Empty Vessel, governing a world he has been trained not to understand.

He is the perfect valedictorian of the End of Knowing.
