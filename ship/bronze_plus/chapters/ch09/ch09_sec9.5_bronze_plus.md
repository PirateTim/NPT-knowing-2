#### 9.5 Transition: From the Vault to the Mind
The "Industrialized Simulacrum" is now complete. We have architected a knowledge system that prioritizes the "Version of Record" while allowing the "Vehicle of Evidence" to vanish. We are left with "Ghost Facts"—statistical weights in an AI model that can no longer be verified against a primary source. But the collapse of the vault is merely the precursor to a more intimate erasure.

The same "Optimization" logic that turned the academic record into a "Golden Escalator" of "Plausible Slop" has been turned toward the human subject. If the archive is hollow, the education system must be redesigned to ensure no one is left capable of noticing the void. We move now from the "Simulacrum" of the library to the "War on Knowing" in the classroom.
