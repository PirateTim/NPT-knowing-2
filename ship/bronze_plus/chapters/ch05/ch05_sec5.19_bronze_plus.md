#### 5.19 The Matmos (The Slop Explosion)
"Bullshit is a greater enemy of the truth than lies are."
— Harry Frankfurt, On Bullshit (2005)

"My student brings me their essay, which has been written by AI, and I plug it into my grading AI, and we are free! The 'learning' happens, our superego is satisfied, and we are free now to learn whatever we want."
— Slavoj Žižek (2023)

The "Open Web" did not die in the dark; it drowned in the light. It wasn't censored by a government or hacked by a rogue state. It was simply suffocated by Volume. In 2023, the release of generative AI tools like Midjourney and ChatGPT marked the transition from the "Social Web" (Humans talking to Humans) to the "Synthetic Web" (Machines talking to Machines). The result was the creation of a substance we came to call "Slop"—content that mimics the shape of information but contains zero nutritional value.

To understand the epistemology of Slop, we must turn to the philosopher Harry Frankfurt. In his seminal essay On Bullshit, Frankfurt draws a critical distinction between the Liar and the Bullshitter. The Liar cares about the Truth; he must know it in order to conceal it. The Bullshitter, however, is indifferent to the Truth. He does not care if his statement describes reality; he only cares if it achieves his purpose [Frankfurt, Harry G. (2005), On Bullshit, Princeton University Press, ISBN: 978-0-691-12294-6, URL: https://press.princeton.edu/books/hardcover/9780691122946/on-bullshit]. Generative AI is the ultimate Bullshitter. When a Large Language Model (LLM) answers a query, it is not "lying" because it has no concept of truth. It is simply predicting the next statistically likely token. It is generating "plausible text" based on a probability distribution.

The result of unleashing this Bullshitter at scale was "Shrimp Jesus". In early 2024, Facebook feeds were flooded with AI-generated images of Jesus Christ constructed from shrimp, spaghetti, or plastic bottles. These images received millions of "Likes" and "Amens" in the comments. But forensic analysis revealed a terrifying reality: the "Users" liking the images were bots. The "Creators" posting the images were scripts. It was Machines High-Fiving Machines. A bot farm in Vietnam generated the Slop to game the algorithm; a bot farm in Russia "Liked" the Slop to build account history; the algorithm promoted the Slop to "Trending" because the metrics were going up [Koebler, Jason (2024), "Where Facebook's AI Slop Actually Comes From", 404 Media, URL: https://www.404media.co/where-facebooks-ai-slop-comes-from/]. The human user was no longer the participant; they were the host body for a parasitic transaction. The "Open Web" had become a "Signal-Free Zone." It was a Zombie Scroll—a closed loop of synthetic noise designed to simulate a culture that no longer existed.
