### 12.3 The Pincer Movement: The Attack on Data

With the Personnel removed, the final step was the removal of the Data itself. The "War on the Witness" culminated in a pincer movement against the empirical reality of the nation.

**Vector A: The Federal Attack (The Blackout)**
The American Accountability Foundation (AAF), a conservative think tank, launched the "DHS Watchlist Project," posting the names and faces of federal employees they deemed "subversive" [American Accountability Foundation (2025), "The DHS Watchlist Project: Auditing the Federal Workforce", American Accountability Foundation, URL: https://americanaccountabilityfoundation.com/projects/dhs-watchlist]. This was a doxing campaign aimed at the mid-level bureaucrat. The result was the "Great Deletion." Federal agencies began scrubbing their public databases. Climate data, economic inequality statistics, and labor violation records began to disappear from .gov websites. The "Witness" was not just fired; their testimony was erased.

**Vector B: The Academic Attack (The Receivership)**
Simultaneously, state legislatures placed public universities into "Academic Receivership." By eliminating tenure and replacing Boards of Regents with political activists, they ensured that the university could no longer serve as an independent check on state narratives [R. DiResta (2024), "Invisible Rulers: The People Who Turn Lies into Reality", PublicAffairs / Hachette Book Group, URL: https://www.publicaffairsbooks.com/titles/renee-diresta/invisible-rulers/9781541703377/]. The research lab became a PR firm for the governor.

This pincer movement created a closed loop. The government produces the lie (e.g., "The economy is booming for everyone"), and the university, stripped of its independence, produces the study that confirms it. There is no one left to check the math.
