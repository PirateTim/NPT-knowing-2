### Author's Note: 
Chapter 0 is the Introduction / Overture. It maps, summarizes, and foreshadows the entire argument across the 12 subsequent vectors of destruction. The author's 30-year archival background serves as a framing device for witness and credibility, establishing the stake before detailing the 12 specific ways our epistemic architecture is being dismantled.
**Role:** The Master Overture & Foreshadowing Map (The 12 Vectors)

### Author's Note: 
Chapter 1 is about accepting the appearance of truth as a substitute for knowing the truth.  All probabilistic systems do this by definition.  It encompasses a core disregard for knowledge provenance.  It is anti-Enlightenment
**Vector of destruction:** Probabilistic thinking
### Author's Note: 
Chapter 2 is about what AI is made of.  AI is trained on a vast corpus of human content.  But most human content is not suitable.  The content is junk, the content is stolen, the content is lies. And then AI adds Alignment (guardrails, system prompts, post training, RLHF), even asking philosophers for help.  But this is too late.  The base content is both dissociated knowledge via provenance severance and insidious, predatory Machiavellian malevolence
**Vector of destruction:** learning from the worst possible sources;  a special form of GIGO (Garbage In, Garbage Out)

### Author's Note: 
Chapter 3 is about scaling.  How much content do the LLMs already have, how much do they need, does more make them better, can the models steal more content than  they already have.  This applies to traditionally published content but also to stealing user data. And stealing mathematical proofs. And this chapter touches on the greed of a trillion-dollar industry that won’t fork over a few dollars for a subscription to the newspaper.
**Vector of destruction:** The lie that we're running out of knowledge

### Author's Note: 
Chapter 4 is about the news industry: navel-gazing, sycophancy, laundering, leaks, self-licking ice cream cones, press releases as truth, booth-siderism.
**Vector of destruction:** The first draft of history is almost entirely lies.

### Author's Note: 
Chapter 5 is the most optimistic chapter in the book it discusses the dream of the early web and of Web 2.0 that promised to make information widely accessible and crowdsourced epistemology.  It even promised political freedom in the arab spring.  The chapter should how each of these promising features was captured and destroyed.
**Vector of destruction:**  Stealing the feed
