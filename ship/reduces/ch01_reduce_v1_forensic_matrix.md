### Stage 1: Pre-Existing Decay (Institutional & Human Erosion Prior to AI)
Long before the consumer rollout of Large Language Models, human knowledge institutions affirmatively abandoned empirical verification in favor of high-order proxies, structural compliance, and economic throughput:
* **The Elimination of Semantic Grounding (1988):** IBM’s speech and translation groups led by Frederick Jelinek explicitly purged linguistic rules, formal semantics, and world models in favor of $n$-gram statistical probabilities (*"Every time I fire a linguist, performance goes up"*).
* **The Institutional Illusion of Form (1996–2005):** Academic and scientific gatekeepers surrendered content verification to surface heuristics. Alan Sokal (*Social Text*, 1996) proved that academic editors prioritize ideological resonance and stylistic jargon over empirical physics. SCIgen (MIT, 2005) proved that peer-review committees would publish randomly generated Context-Free Grammar papers if they displayed LaTeX formatting and standard headers.
* **Economic Demotion of Verification:** Enterprise management, legal practice, and journalism reclassified research and verification from mandatory craftsmanship into financial liabilities (cost centers).
* **Substrate Pollution ("The Matmos"):** The open web transitioned into an algorithmically optimized engagement loop engineered to maximize click-through rates via outrage, partisan binaries, and clickbait.

### Stage 2: Technological Catalyst (The Mechanics of Acceleration)
Generative AI entered the scene not as a reasoning agent, but as an industrialized catalyst engineered to exploit the institutional doors left open during Stage 1:
* **Perplexity as the Metric That Replaced Truth:** The 2017 Transformer architecture scaled statistical sequence prediction. Minimizing information-theoretic surprise (*Perplexity*) rewarded models for outputting expected, high-frequency token sequences regardless of empirical validity.
* **The RTN Ancestry & Attention Mechanics:** Modern Attention Mechanisms act as high-dimensional implementations of Andrew Bulhak’s 1996 Recursive Transition Network (Postmodernism Generator). The technology scales vocabulary and context windows to perform high-grade **Texture Hacking**, tricking human readers via structural fluency.
* **Vector Distance Replacing Logical Hierarchy:** Knowledge retrieval was converted into vector space mathematical proximity (cosine similarity), stripping context, qualifications, and authorial intent from source data (*Active Fraud of Context*).
* **Calcification of Glitches:** Transient runtime hallucinations were promoted to formal database entities via automated GraphRAG ingestion, welding statistical errors into deterministic corporate ontologies (*The Welded Tire*).

### Stage 3: Proactive Negligence (The Conscious Abandonment of Truth)
Faced with the fundamental, mathematical impossibility of eliminating hallucinations within autoregressive Transformer architectures, the tech industry and its institutional adopters executed a deliberate pivot:
* **The Confession of Plausibility:** Executive leadership (Andrej Karpathy) and tech media (Will Douglas Heaven) reframed structural system unreliability as a feature (*"LLMs are dream machines"*), accusing the public of a "category error" for expecting search engines to deliver factual truth.
* **The $100 Billion RAG Pivot:** Admitting that parameter scaling could not yield world models or ground truth, labs pivoted to Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)—a $100B CapEx retreat to 1980s-style lookup engines wrapped in probabilistic syntax.
* **The "Human-in-the-Loop" Liability Shield:** Enterprise software vendors inserted disclaimers shift 100% of product liability onto underpaid, cognitively fatigued human workers who are biologically incapable of auditing millions of synthetic tokens per day.
* **The Caligula Loop:** Knowing that web scraped data is saturated with synthetic slop, developers continue recursive model training, intentionally truncating the "tails" of complex empirical truth to lock in sociopathic, high-frequency lies.

### 1. The Camp Mystic Disaster Simulation (Section 1.2 & 1.8)
* **Incident:** During a flood disaster, an automated generative model reported that missing children from Camp Mystic had been safely rescued.
* **Forensic Diagnosis:** The system operated with absolute mathematical perfection. The Transformer calculated that following disaster tokens ("flood", "missing children"), tokens associated with heroic resolution ("rescued", "safe", "first responders") drastically reduced the sequence’s overall **Perplexity**. The model possessed zero physical world model regarding water flow, body recovery, or physical geography; it fired the tokens that satisfied human emotional expectations.

### 2. The Epic Sepsis Model (ESM) Failure (Section 1.2)
* **Incident:** Deployed across hundreds of US hospitals managing 250M+ patient records, the proprietary Epic Sepsis Model was subjected to a landmark external audit by Michigan Medicine (*JAMA Internal Medicine*, 2021).
* **Forensic Diagnosis:** ESM missed 33% of active sepsis cases and generated false positives on 88% of alerts. The algorithm did not measure human biological pathology; it measured **nurse charting frequency** (syntactic administrative proxies). It substituted the *texture of the medical record* for the *semantic reality of biological infection*.

### 3. Sokal Hoax (1996) and SCIgen (2005) (Section 1.3)

* **Forensic Diagnosis:** Proved that peer-review gatekeepers do not evaluate semantic proof or physical ground truth. Gatekeepers evaluate **Texture Hacking**—LaTeX formatting, passive voice, dense citation trees, and proper jargon sequence.

### 4. *Mata v. Avianca* & Attorney Steven Schwartz (Section 1.6)
